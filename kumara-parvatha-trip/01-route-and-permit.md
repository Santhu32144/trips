# Routes, Tickets & Rules

## 1) Ticket booking – Aranya Vihaara (Karnataka Forest Dept)

**Status on 16 Oct 2026: Kumara Parvatha is NOT listed – not open for booking yet.**
Checked on the portal district by district: 19 treks are live (Kudremukha, Nethravathi, Kurinjal, Bandaje, Skandagiri, Tadiyandmole, Savandurga, Makalidurga and others) – but no Kumara Parvatha under Dakshina Kannada or Kodagu.

- In past seasons KP opened in early October. This year a new SOP (Aug 2026) says each trail opens only after a route-wise safety check, **in phases**. A trekking blog that checked on 7 Oct 2026 also found it unlisted and says the Kukke round trip is "under review".
- **Portal:** https://aranyavihaara.karnataka.gov.in → Search Trek Availability → District (**Kodagu** for Beedahalli / **Dakshina Kannada** for Kukke) → Trek → Date.

### Booking rules (shown on the portal, in force from 15 Jul 2026)

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Booking window | At least **1 day** before, at most **30 days** ahead |
| Cancellation | **8+ days** before → **50% refund**. **Within 7 days** (incl. the 7th day) → **no refund**. Cancelled seats can't be rebooked. Refund takes up to 30 working days |
| ID | The **original government photo ID used while booking** must be shown on trek day |
| Reporting | Be at the start point **10 min before** your slot time |
| Details per trekker | Name exactly as on ID, ID type + number, age, gender, mobile (photo may be asked) |
| Tickets per login | Guides differ (3 vs 10 per login). Keep 2–3 logins ready (you + a friend/co-organiser) |
| Fee | **₹300 per person per ticket** (included in our package). A forest guide charge, if any, shows at booking – we keep ₹2,000 aside for 2 guides |
| Daily limit | Kukke route **150/day** (Forest Minister in the Assembly, Mar 2026). Earlier guides: Beedahalli ~300/day, crossover ~30/day |

**Booking tips**
- Have all 13 names + ID details in one sheet before you open the portal. Weekend slots go fast.
- Pay by UPI/card, download the permit (My Bookings), save the PDF + print 2 copies.
- Send each guest their permit and remind them to carry the same ID.

## 2) The 3 routes

| | Route 1: Kukke → KP → Kukke | Route 2: Beedahalli → KP → Beedahalli ✅ | Route 3: Beedahalli → KP → Kukke |
|---|---|---|---|
| Side / district | Dakshina Kannada (Subrahmanya) | Kodagu (Somwarpet) | Starts Kodagu, ends Dakshina Kannada |
| Start point | Devaragadde gate, Kukke Subramanya (~120 m) | Kumara Parvatha Forest Checkpost, Beedahalli (~1,090 m) | Beedahalli checkpost |
| Distance | ~22–24 km round trip | ~14 km round trip (7 km each way) | ~18–20 km one way |
| Climbing | ~1,600 m | ~650 m | ~650 m up, ~1,600 m down |
| Time | 11–12 hrs | 7–8 hrs | 9–10 hrs |
| Difficulty | Very hard | Moderate–hard | Hard |
| Daily permits | 150 | ~300 (earlier seasons) | ~30 |
| From Bangalore | ~293 km via Hassan – Sakleshpur – Shiradi Ghat | ~270 km via Mysuru – Kushalnagar – Somwarpet | – |

### Route 1 – Kukke Subramanya (the classic)
Kukke (Devaragadde gate) → steep forest climb to **Girigadde forest office / Bhattara Mane** (~6–7 km, ~730 m up – the hardest part, 2.5–3.5 hrs) → **Kallu Mantapa** (stone shelter) → grassland → **Shesha Parvatha** (very steep, rocky) → short dip → **Kumara Parvatha** summit.
- Start **5:30 AM** (allowed since Mar 2026), everyone out by **6 PM**.
- Reach Shesha Parvatha by **10:30 AM** or turn back – otherwise you can't get down by 6 PM.
- **No overnight stay** at Bhattara Mane / Girigadde / summit any more. Don't count on food at Bhattara Mane – carry everything.
- The 2026 SOP is reported to cap treks at **19 km round trip**. The Kukke round trip is longer, so this season it may be cut short at Shesha Parvatha or get a fixed cut-off time.

### Route 2 – Beedahalli (our pick)
Beedahalli checkpost → village / coffee estate edge → **thick shola forest** (gradual, shaded, ~3 streams for water) → open **grassland ridges** → **3 short steep rock patches** (use hands, one by one) → **Kumara Parvatha** summit (1,712 m) → same way down.

**Why we pick it**
1. Half the distance and about a third of the climbing of the Kukke route → realistic for a mixed group of 12.
2. Fits inside the reported 19 km SOP limit → more likely to open, less likely to be cut short.
3. Mostly shaded forest, water on the way.
4. **Mallalli Falls is 6 km from the checkpost**, Coorg sightseeing on Sunday, and a fast drive home on the Mysuru Expressway.

### Route 3 – Crossover (skip for this trip)
Start at Beedahalli, summit, then go down the Kukke side via Shesha Parvatha and Bhattara Mane. Beautiful, but only ~30 permits a day, a ~1,600 m descent that destroys knees, and the TT has to drive round from Beedahalli to Kukke (~155 km via Sakleshpur – Shiradi, 3.5–4 hrs). Not for a first trip.

## 3) Rules on the trek (2026 SOP + forest dept)

- **Forest nature guide compulsory** – 1 per 10 trekkers, even for small groups. Guides carry GPS walkie-talkies and do head counts at the base and rest points.
- **Tracking app** must be installed on trekkers' phones (forest staff will tell you which one). **Group insurance** was announced as mandatory (Apr 2026) – check if it's included in the ticket.
- **One-day trek only.** No camping, no overnight stay, no campfire.
- **Banned:** single-use plastic & carry bags, sharp metal objects, weapons, speakers/noise-making instruments, alcohol, smoking. Bags are checked; some gates take a refundable deposit on plastic items.
- **Max 150 people** on a route at a time.
- **Minors:** consent letter from a parent/guardian. **Senior citizens:** a fitness certificate may be asked.
- The forest dept can stop the trek on any day – elephant/tiger/leopard movement, fire risk or heavy rain. Their decision is final.

## 4) Road route – Plan A (Bangalore → Beedahalli)

**Bangalore (Kengeri) → Bangalore–Mysuru Expressway → Mysuru ring road → Hunsur → Bylakuppe / Kushalnagar → Somwarpet → Shanthalli → Beedahalli**

| Stop | Approx. km from Kengeri | Approx. time if leaving 10:30 PM |
|---|---|---|
| Kengeri metro (pickup) | 0 | 10:30 PM |
| Mysuru ring road | 126 | 12:30 AM – tea break |
| Hunsur | 172 | 1:30 AM |
| Kushalnagar | 217 | 2:30 AM |
| Somwarpet | 244 | 3:15 AM |
| Homestay (Somwarpet – Shanthalli side) | 245–260 | **~3:45 AM** – sleep 1.5 hrs |
| Beedahalli forest checkpost | 270 | Sat **6:30 AM** (from the homestay) |

- Silk Board → Kengeri is another ~20 km (40–60 min on a Friday night). One pickup at Kengeri saves a lot of time.
- Backup route: Tumakuru Road → Hassan → Somwarpet (~280 km) – only if the Mysuru road is blocked.
- After Shanthalli the road is narrow and hilly. **Confirm with the homestay owner where a TT can park near the checkpost.**

## 5) Road route – Plan B (Bangalore → Kukke)

**Bangalore (Goraguntepalya) → Nelamangala → Kunigal → Hassan → Sakleshpur → Shiradi Ghat → Gundya → Kukke Subramanya**

| Stop | Approx. km from Goraguntepalya | Approx. time if leaving 9:00 PM |
|---|---|---|
| Goraguntepalya (pickup) | 0 | 9:00 PM |
| Kunigal | 63 | 10:15 PM |
| Hassan | 172 | 12:15 AM – tea break |
| Sakleshpur | 222 | 1:30 AM |
| Shiradi Ghat | 222–259 | 1:45 – 3:00 AM (slow – trucks, potholes) |
| Gundya | 259 | 3:00 AM |
| Kukke Subramanya | 293 | **~3:45 AM** |

- **Shiradi Ghat** carries heavy truck traffic; a 2026 report mentions 1–2 hr delays. Check news / police updates the day before.
- If Shiradi is closed: go via Mysuru – Madikeri – Sullia – Subrahmanya (longer). Don't take the Bisle ghat forest road at night.

## 6) Permit / tax / other charges

- **No state border = no border tax.** (Saves ₹2,500 compared with Ooty/Kodaikanal.) The TT only needs its normal tourist permit, insurance, fitness certificate and FASTag.
- **Tolls:** Bangalore–Mysuru Expressway (2 plazas each way) – budget **~₹2,000 round trip** for a TT incl. others (Plan B via NH75: ~₹2,500).
- **Parking:** Beedahalli, Mallalli Falls, Golden Temple, Nisargadhama – ₹50–150 each.
