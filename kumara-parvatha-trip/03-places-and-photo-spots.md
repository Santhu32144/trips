# Places & Photo Spots

## The trek – Route 2 (Beedahalli) at a glance

| Section | What it's like | Approx. time going up |
|---|---|---|
| Checkpost (~1,090 m) → forest edge | Village / coffee-estate edge, easy | 15–20 min |
| Shola forest | Thick, shaded, gradual climb over roots and rocks. ~3 streams (water). Leeches possible after rain | 2–2.5 hrs |
| Grassland ridges | Open, windy, full sun – the big views start here | 45 min |
| Rock patches | 3 short, steep rocky climbs – hands needed, one person at a time | 30–45 min |
| **Summit (1,712 m)** | Small rocky top. Shesha Parvatha next door, Pushpagiri forests and Bisle ranges all around | – |

Coming down takes ~3–3.5 hrs. Most slips happen on the rock patches and the forest roots going down.

## The trek – Route 1 (Kukke) at a glance

| Section | What it's like | Approx. time going up |
|---|---|---|
| Kukke (~120 m) → Girigadde / Bhattara Mane (~850 m) | Steep, humid forest – the hardest part | 2.5–3.5 hrs |
| → Kallu Mantapa | Old stone shelter, grassland starts | 45 min |
| → Shesha Parvatha | Steep, rocky, exposed | 1–1.5 hrs |
| → Kumara Parvatha summit | Short dip, then the final rocky climb | 45–60 min |

Coming down takes ~5 hrs. Total ~11–12 hrs.

## After-trek places – Plan A (Coorg side)

| Place | What it is | Time needed | Approx. fee | Timings / best time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mallalli Falls** | Kumaradhara river falling through Pushpagiri forest. Long flight of steps down (and back up!) | 1–1.5 hr | Small panchayat fee + parking | ~9 AM – 5 PM, morning best |
| **Golden Temple – Namdroling Monastery** (Bylakuppe) | Huge Tibetan Buddhist monastery with giant golden Buddha statues | 1 hr | Free | ~9 AM – 6 PM |
| **Kaveri Nisargadhama** | River island – bamboo groves, hanging bridge, deer park | 1 hr | ~₹10–50 + parking | ~9 AM – 5 PM |
| Dubare Elephant Camp (optional) | Elephant bathing & feeding on the Kaveri | 2 hrs | Activities ₹700+ | ~8:30 – 11 AM only |
| Harangi Dam (optional) | Backwaters, short stop | 30 min | Small fee | Afternoon |
| Kote Betta (optional) | Hilltop viewpoint | 2 hrs | – | Sunrise |

## After-trek places – Plan B (Kukke side)

| Place | What it is | Time needed | Approx. fee | Timings / best time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kumaradhara Snana Ghatta** | Holy river bathing ghat at Kukke – same river as Mallalli Falls | 45 min | Free | Early morning |
| **Kukke Subramanya Temple** | Famous Subramanya temple | 1 hr+ (queues) | Free (sevas extra) | ~6 AM – 1:30 PM, 3:30 – 8 PM (timings vary – confirm) |
| **Adi Subramanya** | Older shrine close to the main temple | 20 min | Free | Morning |
| **Manjarabad Fort** | 8-pointed star-shaped fort near Sakleshpur, ~250 steps | 45 min | Free | 8 AM – 6 PM (gate may close ~5 PM) |
| **Shettihalli Rosary Church** | Church built by French missionaries in the 1860s, now ruins in the Hemavathi backwaters. In Nov the backwaters are usually full – the church stands in water | 45 min | Free | Late afternoon light |

## Top photo spots 📸
1. **Kumara Parvatha summit** – group shot with Shesha Parvatha behind.
2. **Grassland ridge** on the way up – the whole group in a line on the ridge.
3. **Shola forest streams** – moss, roots, light rays.
4. **Mallalli Falls** from the top viewpoint.
5. **Golden Temple** – the front with the prayer wheels.
6. **Nisargadhama hanging bridge**.
7. Plan B: **Shesha Parvatha ridge**, **Manjarabad Fort** from the rampart, **Shettihalli Church** reflection in the water.

## Shopping
- **Coorg coffee powder, pepper, cardamom, honey** – Somwarpet / Kushalnagar shops
- **Tibetan handicrafts, momos, Tibetan bread** – Bylakuppe
- Plan B: Kukke prasada; Sakleshpur coffee & spices
- Coorg homemade wines are sold everywhere – **not allowed in the TT** (trip rule)

## Rules & etiquette
- **Trail:** stay behind the forest guide, no shortcuts, no shouting, no plastic, carry every wrapper back.
- **Mallalli Falls:** stay on the steps, **no getting into the water** – people have drowned here.
- **Golden Temple:** dress modestly, footwear off inside, silence in the prayer hall.
- **Kukke temple:** men – dhoti/pancha, shirt off for the inner darshan; women – saree/churidar. No shorts.
- **Nisargadhama:** don't feed the deer; only a few people at a time on the hanging bridge.
- **Shettihalli:** don't climb the ruined walls.
