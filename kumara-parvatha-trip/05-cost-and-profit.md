## Assumptions
- TT with **12 passenger seats + driver. All 12 seats are for paying guests.** You (trip leader) sit in front next to the driver.
- TT rate **₹18/km**, driver bata **₹500/day × 3 days**. Billed **750 km** (250 km/day minimum; actual ~610–650 km).
- **No state border → no permit/border tax.**
- Stay, food and tickets for **13 people** (12 guests + you). Driver's food is covered by bata.
- **KP forest ticket: ₹300 per person.** We keep ₹2,000 for 2 forest guides in case the guide fee is charged separately – remove it if the guide is included in the ticket.
- Included: travel, forest ticket + forest guide, shared homestay, campfire, 4 meals, Sunday entry fees, e-certificate (free).

## Break-even
Fixed costs (TT, tolls, guides) don't go down if fewer people come. Each missing guest saves only ~₹1,610 (stay + food + forest ticket + entry).

| Paying guests | Approx. cost | Revenue at ₹4,199 | Profit / Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 44,530 | 50,388 | **+5,900** |
| 11 | 42,920 | 46,189 | **+3,300** |
| 10 | 41,310 | 41,990 | **+700** |
| 9 | 39,700 | 37,791 | **–1,900 (loss)** |

→ **Minimum 10 bookings** before you pay the TT advance (the brochure says the same).

## What's NOT included (tell guests clearly)
- Friday dinner, Sunday lunch & Sunday dinner
- Trek snacks, drinks, personal expenses
- Insurance of any kind (personal accident / medical cover recommended)
- Activities not listed – rafting, Dubare elephant camp, jeep rides, camera fees
- Extra costs from medical emergencies, road blocks, weather or forest closures

## Payment & cancellation policy (same as the brochure)
- **₹2,000 advance** by UPI to block a seat. Fully refundable if we can't get forest tickets for the date (or shift to the next batch).
- Once tickets are booked, the advance is **non-refundable**. **Balance 7 days before** departure.
- Guest cancels **8+ days** before: everything except the advance is refunded (the forest dept returns only 50% of the ticket).
- Guest cancels **within 7 days**: no refund. **Transfer only if a new ticket can be booked** in the new person's name – they pay ₹300.
- Forest dept closes the trek on the day: the trip runs as a Coorg sightseeing trip. Any ticket refund from the forest dept is passed on.
- We cancel a batch (fewer than 10 bookings, red alert, no tickets): full refund or shift to the next batch.
