# Itinerary – Friday 13 Nov Night to Sunday 15 Nov Night (target date)

> Main plan = **Plan A (Beedahalli route)**. Plan B (Kukke route) is further down – use it only if the group is fit and the Kukke route is open up to the summit.
> The trek start time depends on the slot printed on your permit. Report **10 min before** the slot and shift the Saturday morning times to match.

## Optional / alternate spots

**Plan A (Coorg side)**
- **Dubare Elephant Camp** (15 km from Kushalnagar) – elephant bathing & feeding only ~8:30–11 AM. Swap with Mallalli Falls if the group prefers elephants. Activity tickets extra.
- **Harangi Dam** (8 km from Kushalnagar) – backwaters, 30-min stop.
- **Kote Betta** (23 km from Somwarpet) – hilltop sunrise view. Only if people still have energy.
- **Abbey Falls / Raja's Seat** (Madikeri, ~40 km) – needs 2–3 extra hours. Not in this plan.
- Coffee-estate walk + Coorg coffee / honey / spice shopping in Somwarpet or Kushalnagar.

**Plan B (Kukke side)**
- **Dharmasthala** (~50 km from Kukke) – opposite direction; only if the group wants a temple Sunday.
- **Bisle Viewpoint** – great view of the KP and Pushpagiri ranges, but on a narrow forest road – small vehicles in daylight only. Skip with a TT.

## Rain / closure plan

- **Forest dept closes the trek on the day** (wildlife, rain): you can't enter – don't argue at the gate. Plan A backup: Mallalli Falls + Kote Betta on Saturday, Dubare + Golden Temple + Nisargadhama on Sunday. Refund rules in [05-cost-and-profit.md](05-cost-and-profit.md).
- **Light rain:** the trek can go on, but the rock patches get slippery – the forest guide decides; stop below the rocks if needed.
- **Thunder or lightning near the top:** don't go up the open ridge – turn back.
- **Mallalli Falls** may limit entry after heavy rain – then do Harangi Dam or Dubare.
