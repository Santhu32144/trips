# Stay & Food

## Plan A – Homestay near Somwarpet (Sat 14 Nov night + early check-in Sat ~3:45 AM)

**What to look for**
- Homestay / estate stay for **13 people** (12 guests + you) – dorm-style or 3–4 rooms, extra mattresses ok.
- **Early check-in at ~3:45 AM Saturday** – rooms (or at least washrooms + a hall to rest). Pay a little extra (₹1,500–2,500) or book the Friday night too. The **driver** also sleeps here while you trek.
- **5:30 AM breakfast + 13 packed lunches** on Saturday – many homestays won't do this unless asked. Confirm in writing.
- **Location:** Somwarpet – Shanthalli – Beedahalli belt (closer to the checkpost = more sleep). Search "homestay near Mallalli falls", "homestay Shanthalli", "homestay Somwarpet group".
- **TT access & parking** – estate roads can be narrow and steep. Ask: "Can a 12-seater Tempo Traveller reach your gate?"
- **Hot water / geyser** – everyone wants a hot bath after the trek.
- **Campfire allowed** (~₹500, weather permitting).
- **Driver room** – he needs proper sleep on Saturday night.
- **Separate rooms for girls** in a mixed group.
- Ask the owner **where the TT can park at Beedahalli** and for the local forest office number – locals know the latest rules.

**Budget:** ~₹700 per head (shared rooms, separate for men and women) = **₹9,100** for 13 + early check-in ₹1,500 + driver room ₹500 + campfire ₹500. Ask for a package price with the 4 meals (~₹1,250 per head) – often cheaper.

**How to book:** call directly (pick by Google Maps reviews). Pay 25–50% advance **only after permits are booked**. Get written WhatsApp confirmation: dates, early check-in time, rooms, 5:30 AM breakfast, packed lunches, campfire, geyser, driver room, TT parking.

## Plan B – Lodge in Kukke Subramanya (Fri night + Sat night)

- Kukke is a temple town with many budget lodges on Car Street / near the temple: 4 rooms (3–4 sharing), **₹1,200–2,000 per room per night**.
- Book **2 nights** (Friday-night arrival at ~3:45 AM + Saturday night). Arriving at 4 AM without a room = 13 people with nowhere to change.
- Temple-run guest houses are cheaper but hard to pre-book for groups – don't depend on them.
- Weekends and festival days fill up fast – book as soon as the permits are done.
- No campfire in town lodges.

Trek snacks are **not included** – guests bring dry fruits, chikki, energy bars and Glucon-D/ORS (it's on the brochure checklist). Keep a few ORS sachets in your first-aid kit for emergencies.

**Plan B food:** Kukke is mostly pure-veg (temple town) with cheap meals everywhere. The temple serves free anna-prasada (lunch/dinner) – follow the queue and the rules. Non-veg only on Sunday at Sakleshpur/Hassan.

## Trek-day food tips
- Breakfast must be early but solid – carbs, not oily.
- **ORS / Electral every 2 hrs** – cramps are the most common problem on KP.
- **2 L water** per person (3 L on the Kukke route). Refill at the streams (purification tablets optional).
- Ask guests at booking: **veg / non-veg / allergies**.
- One small garbage bag in every daypack – every wrapper comes back down.
