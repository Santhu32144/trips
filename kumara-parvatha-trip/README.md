# Kumara Parvatha Weekend Trek – Bangalore (Trip Guide Plan)

**Trip type:** Group trek, 12 seats, Tempo Traveller (TT)
**Target dates:** Friday 13 Nov night → Sunday 15 Nov night (trek day Sat 14 Nov, 1 night homestay) – **fix the date only after forest permits are booked**
**Peak:** Kumara Parvatha (Pushpagiri), 1,712 m – inside Pushpagiri Wildlife Sanctuary, on the Kodagu – Dakshina Kannada border
**States crossed:** None – Karnataka only. **No border/permit tax.**
**Distance:** ~270 km one way (Kengeri → Beedahalli), ~610–650 km round trip

## ⚠️ Ticket booking status – checked 16 Oct 2026

- Tickets are sold **only online** on the Forest Department's **Aranya Vihaara** portal: https://aranyavihaara.karnataka.gov.in – no walk-in tickets at the gate.
- **Kumara Parvatha is NOT open for booking yet.** On 16 Oct 2026 the portal lists only *Nethravathi Trek* under Dakshina Kannada (Kukke side) and only *Tadiyandmole Trek* under Kodagu (Beedahalli side).
- After a new trekking SOP (Aug 2026), trails are being reopened **in phases**, after a safety check of each route. No opening date is announced for KP yet (in recent years it opened in early October).
- **Ticket price: ₹300 per person** – included in our package price.
- **What to do:** check the portal every morning. The day KP appears, book permits for all 13 people. Until then take only refundable pre-registrations (template in [07-guide-checklist.md](07-guide-checklist.md)).

## Quick summary

- **Plan A (main):** Beedahalli route. Stay near Somwarpet. Sunday: Mallalli Falls → Golden Temple (Bylakuppe) → Nisargadhama → home via Mysuru Expressway.
- **Plan B (only if the group is fit AND the Kukke route opens up to the summit):** Kukke route. Stay in Kukke. Sunday: Kumaradhara river + Kukke temple → Manjarabad Fort → Shettihalli Church → home via Hassan.
- **Selling price:** **₹4,199 per head, all-inclusive from Bengaluru** (early bird ₹3,999) – same as the market (Adventure Chaarana sells this trek at ₹4,199). Includes TT, forest ticket + forest guide, shared homestay, campfire, 4 meals (Sat breakfast, packed lunch, dinner + Sun breakfast), Sunday entry fees
- **Your cost per head (12 paying guests):** ~₹3,710 (incl. the ₹300 forest ticket)
- **Expected guide profit:** ~₹5,900 for a full TT (~₹5,100 with 4 early-bird seats)
- **Break-even:** **10 paying guests**
- **Our edge over the ₹4,199 competitors:** 12-seat TT instead of a bus, home on **Sunday night** instead of Monday 4 AM, UPI with no GST/gateway add-ons. For more profit at the same price, use a 17-seater (~₹12,700) – see [05-cost-and-profit.md](05-cost-and-profit.md).

## Most important things (don't skip)

1. **Permits first, everything else later.** Don't pay TT or homestay advance until permits are in hand. Permits are non-refundable within 7 days of the trek (50% back if cancelled 8+ days before).
2. **A permit belongs to one person.** It carries the guest's name and ID number; they must show the **same original ID** at the gate. You can't transfer a permit – a replacement guest needs a new one (only if seats are left).
3. **Forest nature guide is compulsory** – 1 per 10 trekkers (2 for our 13) – plus a tracking app on phones. You are the trip leader on top of them.
4. **Day trek only.** No camping, no campfire in the forest, everyone out by **6 PM**. Our turn-around time: **11:30 AM**, wherever the group is.
5. **Fitness:** KP is one of Karnataka's toughest treks. Screen every guest and ask for a practice hike (Savandurga / Makalidurga are open on the portal now).
6. **No plastic** on the trail – bags are checked at the gate.
7. **Avoid 6–8 Nov** – Deepavali weekend (Naraka Chaturdashi Sun 8 Nov, Balipadyami Tue 10 Nov). Roads, homestays and Kukke will be packed.

> Prices are estimates for Nov 2026. KP ticket taken as ₹300 per person. When the trek opens on the portal, check whether a forest guide charge is added, then fix the final price.
