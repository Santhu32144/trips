# Trip Guide Checklist – Kumara Parvatha

Your job on this trip = **get the permits, pick the right people, keep the group together on the mountain, bring everyone down before 6 PM.**
On the trail the **forest nature guides lead and decide the route**. You are the **trip leader** – you handle your 12 guests: time, food, health, morale and money.

## Right now (KP not open yet)
- [ ] **Check Aranya Vihaara every morning** – District: Kodagu (Beedahalli) and Dakshina Kannada (Kukke) → look for Kumara Parvatha in the trek list. Also search the news for "Kumara Parvatha trek open".
- [ ] Post the **pre-registration** message (template below). Collect ₹2,000 + guest details through a **Google Form** – not in WhatsApp chat (ID numbers are private).
- [ ] Get **2–3 TT quotes** (don't pay yet). Ask every owner:
  - 12+1 seater? Push-back seats? Luggage carrier?
  - ₹18/km – **minimum km per day?** (this trip is only ~610–650 km)
  - Tolls, parking – included or extra?
  - Driver bata ₹500/day × 3 days – agreed?
  - Tourist permit, insurance, fitness certificate, FASTag valid?
  - Driver knows the **Somwarpet – Shanthalli – Beedahalli** road? (Plan B: Shiradi Ghat at night?)
  - Driver rests at the homestay during the trek and picks up at 2:30 PM – ok?
  - Get the deal **in writing on WhatsApp**.
- [ ] Shortlist 2–3 homestays (early check-in + 5:30 AM breakfast + packed lunches + campfire + TT access).
- [ ] Tell guests to start walking / climbing stairs now. Suggest a **practice trek** – Savandurga or Makalidurga (both bookable on the portal now).
- [ ] If you can, **do Route 2 yourself once** before the group trip – or at least talk to the homestay owner and the local forest office about the trail.

## 2 weeks before
- [ ] **Minimum 10 bookings?** If not – smaller vehicle or move the date (remember: ticket cancellation within 7 days = no refund).
- [ ] WhatsApp group – share itinerary, packing list, rules, fitness tips.
- [ ] Collect: full name, age, phone, emergency contact, **blood group**, ID details, veg/non-veg, medical notes (BP, asthma, heart, knee, recent surgery), past trek experience.
- [ ] Tell guests: minors need a parent's consent letter; seniors may need a fitness certificate.

## 3–5 days before
- [ ] Collect the balance.
- [ ] Re-confirm homestay: arrival ~3:45 AM, 5:30 AM breakfast, 13 packed lunches, campfire, geyser, driver room.
- [ ] Check the portal / forest notices for closures, and the weather for Kodagu. Heavy-rain alert → call the forest office.
- [ ] Share driver name, phone and TT number in the group.
- [ ] Buy ORS, glucose, first-aid items, leech salt/Dettol, garbage bags. (Guests bring their own trek snacks and a steel lunch box.)
- [ ] If the forest dept names the tracking app in advance, ask guests to install it.
- [ ] Remind everyone: **bring the same original ID used for the permit.**

## Trip day (Friday)
- [ ] Reach the pickup 30 min early. Inspect the TT (tyres, seat belts, spare tyre, wipers).
- [ ] **ID check against permits** – every guest shows the ID used for booking.
- [ ] Head count, read the rules, share emergency numbers.
- [ ] Make sure the driver slept in the day.

## Trek day – how to lead the group
- [ ] **Formation:** forest guide 1 in front → strongest guests → slower guests → forest guide 2 → **you at the very back (sweeper)**. Nobody overtakes the front guide; nobody falls behind you.
- [ ] **Buddy pairs** – everyone has a buddy and checks on them at every stop.
- [ ] **Head count** at the checkpost, every rest stop, the summit and the sign-out.
- [ ] **Rest:** 5 min every 45–60 min. Sip water often, ORS every 2 hrs, a snack at every stop.
- [ ] **Watch for:** cramps (ORS + stretch), dizziness/headache (shade, water, glucose), chest pain or breathlessness (stop, sit, tell the forest guide – go down), twisted ankle (crepe bandage, slow descent with support).
- [ ] **Turn-around 11:30 AM** (Plan A) / **Shesha Parvatha by 10:30 AM** (Plan B). No exceptions – say it in the TT, at the gate and on the way up.
- [ ] **Rock patches:** one person at a time, three points of contact, no selfies near edges, others wait in a safe spot.
- [ ] **Weather:** thunder or heavy rain on the open top → don't climb the rocks, turn back.
- [ ] **Wildlife:** elephants and gaur live here – stay quiet, follow the forest guide, never go off the trail.
- [ ] **Going down:** slower, small steps, poles help, no running. Most injuries happen on the descent.
- [ ] **Emergency:** never leave an injured person alone. The forest guide radios the base; one guide + you stay with the person; the other guide takes the group down.
- [ ] **Sign out** with the forest staff before you leave.

## Sunday
- [ ] Slow start – many guests will be sore. Keep the Mallalli steps optional.
- [ ] Leave Nisargadhama by **3:45 PM** to reach Bangalore by ~9 PM.

## Trip rules (read out in the TT)
1. Be on time – the forest slot is fixed, we can't start late.
2. Carry the **same original ID** used for your permit.
3. No plastic, alcohol, smoking or music on the trail. Bags are checked.
4. Always stay **behind the forest guide and ahead of the trip leader**. No shortcuts.
5. **Turn-around time is final** – the mountain will still be there next year.
6. No selfies on rock edges or cliffs.
7. Tell the trip leader at once if you feel pain, dizziness or cramps.
8. Leave no trace – every wrapper comes back down.
9. Respect the driver, the homestay and each other. Any harassment = removed from the trip, no refund.
