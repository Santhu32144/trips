# Packing List

Pushpagiri in mid-November (approx.): **base 20–28°C by day, summit windy and cool, nights at Somwarpet ~14–17°C.** Morning fog and a passing shower are possible; leeches in the forest after rain. The grassland part is in full sun.

## For guests (send on WhatsApp)

**Trek gear**
- [ ] **Trekking shoes with deep grip** – already worn in (new shoes = blisters). Flat-sole sneakers are dangerous on the rock patches
- [ ] 2–3 pairs of thick socks + **leech socks** (optional, ~₹200 online)
- [ ] **20–30 L daypack** with rain cover
- [ ] **2 L water** in 2 steel / hard bottles (3 L for the Kukke route)
- [ ] Cap, sunglasses, **sunscreen**
- [ ] Light **windcheater / rain jacket** or poncho
- [ ] **Headlamp / torch** (must on the Kukke route, backup on Beedahalli)
- [ ] **Trekking pole** (optional – a big help on the way down)
- [ ] Knee cap / crepe bandage if you have knee problems
- [ ] Small towel, tissues, wet wipes, hand sanitizer
- [ ] Salt sachet / Dettol for leeches
- [ ] **Steel lunch box** – the homestay packs your trek lunch in it
- [ ] **Your own trek snacks** – dry fruits, chikki, energy bars, fruit, Glucon-D / ORS (not included in the package)

**Clothes**
- [ ] Quick-dry T-shirt + track pants for trek day (**no jeans** – heavy when wet)
- [ ] A full change of clothes left at the homestay / TT
- [ ] Light jacket / sweater for night and early morning
- [ ] Slippers / floaters for after the trek
- [ ] Night wear

**Documents**
- [ ] **The SAME original ID used for your forest permit** – no ID = no entry
- [ ] Permit copy on your phone (the guide also carries prints)
- [ ] Cash ₹500 in small notes + UPI

**Personal**
- [ ] Personal medicines (inhaler, BP etc.) – tell the trip leader
- [ ] Phone fully charged + **power bank** – the tracking app runs all day
- [ ] Toiletries
- [ ] Neck pillow (night journey)
- [ ] Lip balm, moisturizer

**Don't bring**
- Plastic bottles / covers (checked at the gate), alcohol, cigarettes, speakers, knives, big suitcases – soft bags only

## Guide kit (YOU must carry)
- [ ] **Printed permits (2 sets)** + guest list with ID type/number, phone, emergency contact, blood group, medical notes
- [ ] **Trek first-aid kit:** band-aids (30), blister plasters, cotton, Betadine, antiseptic wipes, **crepe bandages × 3, knee caps × 2**, Volini spray, Dolo 650, Avomine, Digene/Eno, Cetirizine, **ORS × 20, glucose powder**, scissors, gloves, emergency foil blanket, salt pouch
- [ ] **Whistle** (code: 1 blast = stop, 2 = come to me, 3 = emergency)
- [ ] Headlamp + spare batteries
- [ ] **2 power banks** (20,000 mAh)
- [ ] **Cash ₹5,000** (parking, homestay extras, emergency)
- [ ] Extra 2 L water + extra snacks / ORS for slow walkers
- [ ] Garbage bags (everything comes back down)
- [ ] **Offline Google Maps** of the Somwarpet – Kukke area
- [ ] 20 L water can + steel/paper cups in the TT
- [ ] Notebook + pen (expenses, head counts)
- [ ] Spare raincoat
- [ ] Bluetooth speaker, cards, dumb charades list – **for the homestay only, not the trek**
- [ ] Tarpaulin + rope if luggage goes on the TT roof
