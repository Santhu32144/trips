# Packing List

Kodagu in mid-November: **day 22–27°C, night 14–17°C**, misty mornings, hot sun on the grassland, a shower possible. Trek = 14 km, steep.

## For guests (send on WhatsApp)

**Trek gear (most important)**
- [ ] **Trekking shoes / sports shoes with deep grip** – no flat sneakers, no new shoes (blisters)
- [ ] **Day backpack 20–30 L** with rain cover
- [ ] **2–3 litres of water** (steel bottles / hydration bladder) – no streams to refill
- [ ] Trekking pole (strongly recommended for the descent)
- [ ] Cap/hat, sunglasses, **sunscreen** – grassland has no shade
- [ ] Light **raincoat / poncho**
- [ ] Quick-dry T-shirt + track pants (no jeans), extra socks, **leech socks** or salt pouch
- [ ] Empty lunch box (packed lunch is given), energy bars, dry fruits
- [ ] Small torch / headlamp

**Clothes**
- [ ] Light jacket / sweater for the night and the summit wind
- [ ] Change of clothes for after the trek + Sunday, night wear
- [ ] Slippers / sandals for the homestay
- [ ] Polythene-free wet-clothes bag (cloth bag)

**Documents & money**
- [ ] **Original ID** – the same ID used for the forest permit (checked at the gate)
- [ ] Cash + UPI – no network on the trek

**Personal**
- [ ] Personal medicines, inhaler if asthmatic
- [ ] Knee cap if you have knee issues
- [ ] Toiletries, small towel, moisturiser, lip balm
- [ ] Power bank, waterproof phone pouch
- [ ] Neck pillow (overnight drive)

**Don't bring**
- Plastic bottles/covers/wrappers (gate check), alcohol, cigarettes, speakers, big suitcases

## Guide kit (YOU must carry)
- [ ] Printed + phone copy: **all 13 permits**, guest list with ID, phone, emergency contact, blood group, medical notes
- [ ] **Trek first-aid kit:** crepe bandage ×2, Volini/Moov spray, ORS ×15, Dolo 650, Avomine, Cetirizine, Digene, band-aids, blister plasters, Betadine, cotton, scissors, Vicks, Strepsils, glucose, **salt / Dettol for leeches**, emergency foil blanket
- [ ] Whistle, headlamp + spare batteries
- [ ] **Cash ₹5,000** (parking, guide tip, jeep, emergency)
- [ ] Power bank 20,000 mAh
- [ ] 13 trek snack kits (ORS, Glucon-D, chikki, dates, fruit)
- [ ] Garbage bags (carry everything back down)
- [ ] **"Shikhara Trails" A3 banner** for the summit photo + 12 printed summit certificates (give at dinner)
- [ ] Spare raincoat, spare pair of socks
- [ ] Bluetooth speaker (homestay only), cards, games for campfire
- [ ] Notebook + pen (expenses)
- [ ] 20 L water can in the TT
