# Trip Guide Checklist – Kumara Parvatha

## 3–4 weeks before
- [ ] **Check Aranya Vihaara daily** – is Kumara Parvatha (Beedahalli) listed for 14 Nov? **No listing = no poster.**
- [ ] Get **2–3 TT quotes**. Ask every owner:
  - 12+1 seater? Push-back seats? Luggage carrier?
  - ₹18/km incl. rent + driver + diesel – **minimum km/day?** Bata included?
  - Tolls, parking – extra?
  - Can the TT handle narrow Somwarpet roads? Driver done Hassan – Somwarpet before?
  - Insurance, fitness, FASTag valid?
  - Deal **in writing on WhatsApp**.
- [ ] Shortlist 2 homestays (early check-in, packed lunch, hot water, campfire, driver room).
- [ ] Poster + WhatsApp message → bookings. **Collect ID details with the advance.**

## As soon as permit slots open (up to 30 days before)
- [ ] Book permits for all confirmed guests + you, **earliest slot (6:30–7 AM)**.
- [ ] Names exactly as on ID. Save PDFs, share each guest's permit in the group.
- [ ] Late bookings → add permits the same day (slots run out).

## 2 weeks before
- [ ] **10+ bookings?** If not – postpone / smaller vehicle.
- [ ] Pay TT & homestay advance.
- [ ] WhatsApp group: itinerary, packing list, rules, **fitness prep** (walk 5 km daily, climb stairs 10–15 floors).
- [ ] Collect: age, emergency contact, blood group, medical issues (asthma, knee, BP), veg/non-veg.

## 3–5 days before
- [ ] Collect balance.
- [ ] Reconfirm homestay: 4:15 AM arrival, 5:45 AM breakfast, 13 packed lunches.
- [ ] Check weather + forest notices (trek closures after heavy rain).
- [ ] Buy trek snack kits, ORS, first-aid refill; print certificates + banner.
- [ ] Share driver name, phone, vehicle number.

## Trip day (Friday)
- [ ] Reach pickup 30 min early. Inspect TT (tyres, wipers, seat belts, spare tyre).
- [ ] Head count, ID check (same ID as permit!), read the rules, **trek briefing #1**.
- [ ] Driver slept in the day.

## Trek day – your safety system
- [ ] **Forest guide leads, you sweep at the back.** Nobody walks behind you.
- [ ] **Buddy pairs** – each guest knows their buddy.
- [ ] Head count at the checkpost, forest edge, summit, and back at the checkpost.
- [ ] Break every 30–40 min, sip water every break. Watch for cramps, headache, dizziness.
- [ ] **12:30 PM turnaround – no exceptions.** Slow guest at 9:30 AM still in forest → turns back with a buddy (and the forest guide's OK).
- [ ] Rain/lightning on open grassland → move down to the forest, don't shelter under lone trees.
- [ ] Tell the homestay owner + driver your expected return time (4 PM). If you're not back by 6 PM, they call the forest office.
- [ ] Carry all garbage back. Count wrappers.

## Trip rules (read out in the TT)
1. Be on time – TT waits max 10 minutes. On trek morning we **cannot** wait (permit slot).
2. No alcohol / smoking / drugs – whole trip. Gate checks bags; violators are sent back without refund.
3. No plastic in the sanctuary. Carry everything back.
4. Stay with the group and the guide. Never take shortcuts or go off-trail.
5. Respect the turnaround time – the mountain will be there next time.
6. No risky selfies on cliff edges. No entering the water at Mallalli Falls.
7. Respect each other, the driver, the forest staff and the locals.

## After the trip
- [ ] Settle TT + bata (+ tip ₹200–500), forest guide tip.
- [ ] Share photos + **e-certificates**, ask for Google/Instagram reviews.
- [ ] Update actual costs in [costing.csv](costing.csv).

## Emergency numbers
- **All emergencies: 112** · Ambulance **108** · Police **100**
- Forest: Beedahalli checkpost / Pushpagiri WLS range office (save number from the portal / checkpost board)
- Hospitals: **Somwarpet Govt Hospital** (~27 km), **Hassan HIMS** (major), Madikeri District Hospital
- Save: TT owner, driver, homestay owner, a local jeep driver.
