# Stay & Food

## Stay – 1 night (Saturday 14 Nov) + early check-in

**Where:** a homestay in the **Shanthalli – Beedahalli – Heggadahalli belt** (≤15 km from the checkpost) or Somwarpet town (27 km). Not Madikeri / Kushalnagar – too far for a 6:15 AM start.

**What to ask for**
- **13 people (12 guests + guide)**, shared rooms / dorm, **separate room + washroom for women**.
- **Early check-in ~4:15 AM Saturday** for freshen-up and a short rest (we pay for one night – most trek homestays allow this; get it in writing).
- **Breakfast at 5:45 AM** and **13 packed lunches** ready by 6:00 AM.
- **Hot water** after the trek (geyser / firewood boiler) – most important comfort of the trip.
- **Campfire** allowed (~₹1,000).
- **TT parking** + can a 12-seater reach the gate?
- **Driver room** – he drove all night; he sleeps while we trek.
- Network/Wi-Fi (bonus).

**How to book**
- Google Maps "homestay Shanthalli", "homestay near Kumara Parvatha Beedahalli", "Somwarpet homestay group". Call directly for a group rate.
- Budget: **₹800 per head** for the night (≈ ₹10,400) + driver room ₹500 + campfire ₹1,000. Food separate (below) or as a package.
- Pay 25–50% advance, get **written WhatsApp confirmation**: date, early check-in time, rooms, women's room, meal times, packed lunch, hot water, campfire, driver room.

**Tips**
- Ask guests at booking: **veg / non-veg / allergies**. Tell the homestay 3 days before.
- Packed lunch in **steel boxes or foil + paper** – no plastic covers (forest check).
- Light breakfast before the trek – heavy food + steep climb = vomiting.
- Keep one 20 L water can in the TT; refill bottles at the homestay.
