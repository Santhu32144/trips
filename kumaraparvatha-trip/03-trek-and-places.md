# Trek & Places

## Kumara Parvatha trek – Beedahalli route

| | |
|---|---|
| Peak | **Kumara Parvatha (Pushpagiri) – 1,712 m**, Pushpagiri Wildlife Sanctuary, Kodagu / Dakshina Kannada border |
| Start / end | Beedahalli forest checkpost (near Heggadahalli, Somwarpet taluk) – ~970 m |
| Distance | ~7 km up + ~7 km down = **~14 km** |
| Climb | **~750 m** (more with ups and downs) |
| Time | 4–4.5 hrs up, 3–3.5 hrs down, ~30–45 min at the top = **7–8 hrs** |
| Grade | **Moderate – Difficult.** Fit beginners OK. Not for people who can't walk 10 km |
| Best season | Oct – Jan (green after monsoon, clear views). Closed in monsoon & summer fire season |
| Weather mid-Nov | 15–27°C, misty mornings, strong sun on the open grassland by noon, possible shower |

### Trail in sections
1. **Checkpost → forest (0–2.5 km):** shady evergreen forest, steep with roots and rock steps. **Leeches** possible after rain.
2. **Forest edge → grassland ridges (2.5–5.5 km):** open grass slopes, ridge walking, big views. No shade – caps and sunscreen.
3. **Rock slabs + shola patch → summit (5.5–7 km):** last steep push through stunted shola forest. Slippery when wet.
4. **Summit:** 360° views of the Western Ghats – Shesha Parvatha, Kukke valley, Bisle range. Often in the clouds.

**Water:** don't count on streams on this route. **Carry 2–3 L per person** from the homestay.

### Cut-off times (non-negotiable)
- Start 6:45 AM · **Turnaround 12:30 PM** · Back at checkpost by 4:00 PM · Forest exit deadline 6:00 PM.
- If a guest is struggling by 9:30 AM (still in the forest section), the guest + one buddy turn back with the forest guide's OK – don't hold the whole group.

## Day 2 places

| Place | What it is | Time needed | Approx. fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mallalli Falls** | Kumaradhara river drops ~60 m into a forest gorge, foot of Pushpagiri | 1.5–2 hrs | Free / small eco-fee + parking | ~600 steps down & up. Open ~9 AM–5 PM. **No getting into the water** |
| **Manjarabad Fort** | Tipu Sultan's star-shaped fort (1792) on a hill near Sakleshpur | 45–60 min | Free / small fee | ~250 steps. Laterite walls, ghat views |
| Sakleshpur | Malnad lunch stop on NH75 | 1 hr | – | Buy fresh coffee powder here |

## Top photo spots 📸
1. **Grassland ridge walk** – line of trekkers on the ridge with clouds rolling in.
2. **Summit marker** – group photo with the "Shikhara Trails" banner (print one A3 banner, ₹150).
3. **Shola forest** near the top – misty, twisted trees.
4. **Mallalli Falls** viewpoint – waterfall + gorge.
5. **Manjarabad Fort** – the star shape is best from the top of the walls.
6. **Coffee estate at sunrise** near the homestay.

## Shopping
- Coorg / Sakleshpur **coffee powder**, homemade wine (don't open in the TT), spices, honey, cardamom.

## Rules on the trek
- **No plastic** inside the sanctuary – bags are checked; carry back every wrapper.
- **No alcohol / smoking / music speakers.** No fire.
- Stay on the trail; no shortcuts down the grass slopes.
- No feeding animals, no plucking plants.
- Stay with the group – trekkers alone without the guide can be fined/sent back.
