## Most important things (don't skip)

1. **GO / NO-GO: the trek must be live on `aranyavihaara.karnataka.gov.in`.** On 7 Oct 2026 Kumara Parvatha was *not yet listed* (forest dept is reopening treks in phases). Check before posting the poster. If it's not open by **~25 Oct**, shift to 20–22 or 27–29 Nov.
2. **Book all 13 permits the moment slots open** (up to 30 days ahead). Weekend slots fill fast. Each permit needs the guest's **name exactly as on ID, ID number, age, gender, mobile and a photo (<1 MB)** – collect these WITH the advance.
3. **Portal cancellation:** 50% refund if cancelled 8+ days before, **nothing within 7 days** – that's why our advance is non-refundable.
4. **We use the Beedahalli (Somwarpet, Kodagu) route, not Kukke.** The new forest SOP caps treks at 19 km; Kukke round trip (22–28 km) is under review. Beedahalli round trip is ~14 km.
5. **Turnaround cut-off 12:30 PM** – whoever hasn't reached the top turns back. Forest exit deadline is **6 PM**. Overnight stay/camping on the mountain is banned.
6. **No alcohol, no smoking, no plastic** – forest staff check bags at the gate.
7. **Fitness filter:** ask every guest "Can you walk 10 km comfortably?" It's a steep trek – one unfit guest slows all 12.
