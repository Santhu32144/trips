# Route & Permit

## Road route (Karnataka only)

**Bangalore (Tumkur Road side) → Nelamangala → Kunigal → Channarayapatna → Hassan (NH75) → state highways to Shanthalli → Beedahalli / Heggadahalli (Somwarpet taluk, Kodagu)**

No state border, so **no permit, no border tax, no e-pass**. The road after Hassan is a two-lane state highway through coffee country – fine for a TT, a bit narrow and curvy in the last 25 km.

| Stop | Approx. km from Bangalore (Goraguntepalya) | Approx. time if leaving 10:30 PM |
|---|---|---|
| Nelamangala (start of NH75) | 20 | 11:00 PM |
| Kunigal | 65 | 11:45 PM |
| Bellur Cross / Channarayapatna – tea break | 130–145 | 12:45 – 1:15 AM |
| Hassan bypass | 180 | 2:00 AM |
| Shanthalli (homestay area) | ~270 | ~4:00 – 4:30 AM |
| Beedahalli forest checkpost (trek start) | ~285 | Sat 6:30 AM (after freshen-up & breakfast) |

> Reach the homestay by 4:30 AM, not earlier – homestays don't like 3 AM arrivals. If you're early, take a longer tea break at Hassan.

### Day 2 route
Homestay (Shanthalli) → **Mallalli Falls** (11 km) → **Manjarabad Fort**, Sakleshpur (~51 km) → Hassan → Bangalore (~233 km). About 300 km, ~6 hrs driving + stops.

### Why Beedahalli and not Kukke Subramanya?
| | **Beedahalli round trip (our plan)** | Kukke round trip |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | ~14 km | 22–28 km |
| Climb | ~750 m | ~1,600 m |
| Time | 7–8 hrs | 11–13 hrs |
| 2026 status | Fits the new 19 km SOP | **Under review** (exceeds 19 km) |
| Success rate for a mixed weekend group | High | Low – many turn back |
| Start | Beedahalli forest checkpost, Somwarpet | Behind Kukke temple |

Same peak, same grasslands and summit views, half the risk. Crossover (Beedahalli → Kukke, ~18 km) has very few permits (~30/day) and needs the TT to drive round to Kukke – skip it.

## Forest trek permit – Aranya Vihaara

- Portal: **https://aranyavihaara.karnataka.gov.in** (Karnataka Forest Department). Only official way to get a permit.
- **Ticket: ₹300 per person** (as confirmed) – recheck on the portal; some sources say ₹350 + GST.
- Book **at least 1 day and at most 30 days** before the trek date. For Sat 14 Nov, slots open ~15 Oct.
- **Status check (7 Oct 2026):** KP was **not yet listed**; treks are being reopened in phases (Tadiyandamol opened 1 Oct). Keep checking daily – don't take money until it's live.
- Daily caps (last season): ~300/day Beedahalli, ~300/day Kukke, ~30/day crossover. Weekends sell out.
- Per trekker you need: **first name exactly as on ID, ID proof (Aadhaar/DL/Voter ID/PAN/passport), age, gender, mobile, recent photo under 1 MB.**
- **Refund rules:** cancel 8+ days before → 50% back; within 7 days → nothing.
- A **trained forest guide is mandatory** on every trek (they carry a GPS-tracking app). Usually assigned by the forest office – confirm if the fee is extra.
- Report at the checkpost **10 minutes before your slot**. Carry printed + phone copy of permits and **original IDs**.

## Other charges on the way
- **Tolls:** NH75 (Nelamangala tollway + ~3 plazas to Hassan) – budget **~₹1,800 round trip** for a TT.
- **Parking:** checkpost, Mallalli Falls, Manjarabad Fort ₹50–200 each.
- **No** inter-state permit, **no** green tax, **no** e-pass.

## Road notes
- Night drive on NH75 is a good 4-lane up to Hassan. After Hassan: narrow state highways, cattle on road, fog in the early morning – driver must slow down.
- Last stretch to Beedahalli / Mallalli is narrow. Ask the homestay owner if a 12-seater TT can reach the checkpost; if not, the checkpost is ~1.5 km walk from the Heggadahalli road-head, or use a local jeep.
- Network: patchy after Shanthalli; offline-download the Somwarpet area on Google Maps.
