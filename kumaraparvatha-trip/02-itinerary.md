# Itinerary – Friday 13 Nov Night to Sunday 15 Nov Night

## FRIDAY – Departure night

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 9:45 PM | Guide reaches pickup point. Check TT: clean seats, FASTag, first-aid kit, 20 L water can, snack packs loaded |
| 10:00 PM | Guest reporting time – **one** pickup point on the Tumkur Road side (e.g. Goraguntepalya / Yeshwanthpur – straight onto NH75) |
| **10:30 PM** | **Depart** |
| 10:45 PM | Intro round, trip rules, trek briefing #1 (cut-off times, no plastic/alcohol), WhatsApp group check |
| 12:45 AM | Tea break – Bellur Cross / Channarayapatna |
| ~4:15 AM | Reach homestay (Shanthalli area). Freshen up, 1 hr rest |

## SATURDAY – Trek day

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 5:30 AM | Wake up, tea/coffee |
| 5:45 AM | **Breakfast** at homestay. Collect **packed lunch + trek snack kit** (ORS, Glucon-D, chikki, banana). Fill 2–3 L water per person |
| 6:15 AM | Drive to **Beedahalli forest checkpost** (~14 km) |
| 6:35 AM | Permit + ID check, bag check (plastic, alcohol), meet forest guide. Group photo |
| **6:45 AM** | **Start trek** – forest guide in front, **you at the back (sweep)**. Buddy pairs |
| 6:45 – 8:30 AM | Dense evergreen forest – steep, roots and rocks. Leech check at every stop |
| 8:30 – 10:30 AM | Open **grassland ridges** – the classic KP views. Short breaks every 30–40 min |
| **10:45 – 11:30 AM** | **Kumara Parvatha summit – 1,712 m.** Photos, packed lunch, summit certificate photo with the banner |
| **12:30 PM** | **Turnaround cut-off** – everyone starts down, summit or not |
| 12:15 – 3:45 PM | Descent – slower and more slippery than going up. Knees! Use trekking poles |
| ~4:00 PM | Back at checkpost, sign out. Forest exit deadline is 6 PM |
| 4:30 PM | Homestay – hot water bath, tea + pakoda, stretch session |
| 7:30 PM | **Campfire** + games, summit stories |
| 8:30 PM | Dinner (Coorg style, veg + non-veg) |
| 10:00 PM | Lights out. Brief: 7 AM wake-up |

## SUNDAY – Waterfall, star fort, return

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 6:30 AM | Optional: sunrise + coffee estate walk near the homestay |
| 7:45 AM | Breakfast |
| 8:45 AM | Pack up, **checkout by 9:00 AM**. Room check! |
| 9:15 AM | **Mallalli Falls** (11 km) – Kumaradhara river falling ~60 m into a gorge. ~600 steps down to the viewpoint; tired legs can enjoy the top view |
| 11:15 AM | Drive to Sakleshpur (~1.5 hrs) |
| 12:45 PM | **Lunch** – Malnad meals in Sakleshpur |
| 1:45 PM | **Manjarabad Fort** – Tipu Sultan's star-shaped fort (1792), ~250 steps, Western Ghats views |
| **3:00 PM** | **Depart for Bangalore** |
| 4:15 PM | Tea break – Hassan |
| 6:30 PM | Snack break near Kunigal (dinner on own) |
| **~8:30 – 9:00 PM** | **Reach Bangalore** |

## Rain / closure plan
- **Heavy rain on trek morning:** forest guide decides. If the trek is closed, do Mallalli Falls + a coffee estate walk on Saturday, keep Sunday the same. Permit refund follows forest rules.
- **Trek closed before the trip** (forest dept / red alert): full refund or shift date (tell guests clearly – see policy).
- **Mist at summit:** normal. Stay on the trail – grassland ridges have cliff edges on one side.

## Optional spots (if time / group wants)
- **Bisle viewpoint** (Bisle Ghat) – Western Ghats panorama; ~1 hr detour from Sakleshpur side, narrow road.
- **Coffee estate tour** near Somwarpet – ask homestay.
- **Hemavathi backwaters / Shettihalli church** near Hassan – only if the group is fast.
