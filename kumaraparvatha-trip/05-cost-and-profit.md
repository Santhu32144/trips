# Cost & Profit Calculation

## Assumptions
- TT with **12 passenger seats + driver. All 12 seats are for paying guests.** You (guide) sit in front.
- TT rate **₹18/km – includes TT rent, driver and diesel.** Tolls, parking = extra.
- Driver bata **₹500/day × 3 days** (same as Ooty/Kodai). *If your owner's ₹18/km already covers bata, you save ₹1,500.*
- Actual distance: ~285 × 2 + ~30 km local (homestay ↔ checkpost) + Day 2 detours ≈ **630 km**.
- **Billed: 750 km** – minimum 250 km/day × 3 days. If your owner bills actual km, you save ~₹2,100.
- **Trek ticket ₹300 per person** (Aranya Vihaara).
- **No TN permit, no e-pass, no green tax** – Karnataka only.
- Package is **all-inclusive**: travel, homestay, campfire, 5 meals + trek snacks, trek ticket, local guide, Day 2 entries.
- Stay, food and tickets for **13 people** (12 guests + you). Driver's food covered by bata.

## Selling price & your profit (12 paying guests)

| Price per head | Revenue (12 guests) | Cost | **Your profit** |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₹4,699 | 56,388 | 51,250 | **≈ ₹5,100** |
| **₹4,999 (final price)** | 59,988 | 51,250 | **≈ ₹8,700** |
| ₹5,299 | 63,588 | 51,250 | **≈ ₹12,300** |

With early bird (first 4 seats at ₹4,699 = ₹1,200 less) → profit **≈ ₹7,500**.

**Final price: ₹4,999 per head (early bird ₹4,699).**
Why: Bangalore KP packages sell at ₹3,500–4,500 – but those are 30–40-seat buses, veg-only meals, no Sunday lunch, rafting/lunch extra (e.g. one operator's Beedahalli package is ₹4,199). A 12-seat TT can't match bus pricing, so sell the **premium small-group** version: TT, 5 meals with non-veg, campfire, trek snack kit, Day 2 included, summit certificate. "Under ₹5,000" still reads well on Instagram.

## Break-even
Each missing guest saves ~₹2,250 (stay ₹800 + food ₹1,100 + ticket ₹300 + entry ₹50).

| Paying guests | Approx. cost | Revenue at ₹4,999 | Profit / Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 51,250 | 59,988 | **+8,700** |
| 11 | 49,000 | 54,989 | **+6,000** |
| 10 | 46,750 | 49,990 | **+3,200** |
| 9 | 44,500 | 44,991 | **+500** |
| 8 | 42,250 | 39,992 | **–2,300 (loss)** |

→ **Minimum 10 bookings** before you pay the TT advance.

## What's NOT included (tell guests clearly)
- Friday night dinner and Sunday dinner on the highway
- Rafting, coffee estate tour, personal shopping
- Porter / extra guide for a guest who can't continue
- Extra costs due to trek closure, heavy rain or road blocks

## Payment & cancellation policy
- **Advance ₹2,000 per head (non-refundable)** – collected with ID details for the permit. Balance 5 days before.
- Cancel 7+ days before: advance lost, balance refunded. Within 7 days: no refund (transfer allowed **only if the forest portal allows a name change** – usually it doesn't, so warn guests).
- Trip cancelled by you (trek not opened, forest closure, red alert, <10 bookings): full refund or shift date.
- Trek closed by forest dept **on the day**: trip continues with the rain plan; ticket refund only as per forest rules.
