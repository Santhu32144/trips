# Route & Permit

## Best route (Karnataka → Tamil Nadu only)

**Bangalore → Mysore (Expressway) → Nanjangud → Gundlupet → Bandipur (KA) → Mudumalai / Theppakadu (TN) → Gudalur → Naduvattam → Pykara → Ooty**

Only **one state border – Bandipur (Karnataka) / Mudumalai (Tamil Nadu)** at Kakkanahalla check post. Gudalur is next to Kerala, but our road (Gudalur → Naduvattam → Ooty) stays **inside Tamil Nadu** – don't take any turn towards Nadugani / Vazhikkadavu (Kerala).

### Why leave at 11 PM and not 9:30 PM?
The forest gate at Bandipur is **closed from 9 PM to 6 AM**. If you leave at 9:30 PM you'll just wait longer at the gate. Leaving 11 PM gives guests time to reach after office and still makes the 6 AM gate opening. Reaching the gate early (~3:30–5 AM) is good – vehicles queue up and the first ones go through first.

### Why the Gudalur road and not Kalhatti?
- **Kalhatti ghat** (Masinagudi → Ooty) has 36 hairpin bends with very steep gradient. Many accidents; there are restrictions on outside-state/tourist vehicles, especially **going downhill**. Not worth the risk for a TT with 12 people.
- **Gudalur – Naduvattam – Pykara road** is longer by ~20 km, but wider and gentler – AND it passes Pykara Falls, Pykara Lake, Shooting Point and Pine Forest on the way. Free sightseeing!

### Routes to AVOID
- **Via Kerala (Wayanad / Sulthan Bathery):** extra state = extra permit.
- **Via Salem – Coimbatore – Mettupalayam – Coonoor:** only KA → TN too, and no forest timings – but **~520 km one way** (almost double). Use it only if the forest road is closed (landslide/elephant/emergency).

## Return timing (VERY IMPORTANT)
- Forest closes again **9 PM**. Ooty → Bandipur exit gate is ~3.5–4 hrs.
- **Leave Ooty by 1:30 PM Sunday.** Hard deadline 3:00 PM.
- If you miss it, you'll be stuck at the gate till 6 AM Monday – with 12 angry guests. Don't let this happen.

## Ghat road notes
- Gudalur → Ooty has many curves (not as bad as Kalhatti). Give motion-sickness tablets at **Gudalur breakfast**.
- Oct is rainy season in Nilgiris – fog and landslides possible. Check news a day before.
- Inside Bandipur/Mudumalai: **speed limit 30–40 km/h, no stopping, no getting down, no honking, no feeding animals, no photos stopping on road**. Forest guards fine heavily.

## Permit / border tax
Same as any Karnataka tourist TT entering Tamil Nadu:
1. **All India Tourist Permit (AITP)** – usually no extra state tax; OR
2. **TN border/entry tax** paid online on **Vahan** or at the check post.

Ask the TT owner: AITP? Included in ₹18/km or extra? Confirmed: **₹2,500**. Get the receipt on WhatsApp before departure.

## Ooty (Nilgiris) e-pass
- Since 2024, all vehicles entering **The Nilgiris (Ooty)** need an **e-pass** from **https://epass.tnega.org**.
- There may be a **daily vehicle limit on weekends** – apply as early as allowed.
- Needs: vehicle number, driver details, passengers count, dates, stay address. Save the QR on your phone AND driver's phone.

## Other charges on the way
- **Tolls:** Bangalore–Mysore Expressway (2 toll plazas) – TT/LCV ~₹500–600 one way. Budget **~₹2,000 round trip** incl. others.
- **Nilgiris green tax / entry fee** at the check post (~₹100–300 for a TT).
- **Parking** at Botanical Garden, Lake, Doddabetta, Pykara: ₹50–150 each.
