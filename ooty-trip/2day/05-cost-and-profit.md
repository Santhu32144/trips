# Cost & Profit Calculation

## Assumptions
- TT with **12 passenger seats + driver. All 12 seats are for paying guests.** You (guide) sit in front next to the driver.
- TT rate **₹18/km** (includes TT rent + driver salary). Tolls, parking, permit = extra (confirm!).
- Driver bata **₹500/day × 3 days** (Fri, Sat, Sun).
- Actual distance: ~305 × 2 + ~60 km local (Doddabetta, Rose Garden etc.) ≈ **670–700 km**.
- **Billed: 750 km** – most TT owners have a minimum of 250 km/day × 3 days. If your owner has no minimum, you save ~₹1,000.
- Package is **all-inclusive**: travel, stay, 5 meals, entry tickets, boating, bonfire.
- Stay, food and tickets for **13 people** (12 guests + you). Driver's food is covered by bata.

## Selling price & your profit (12 paying guests)

| Price per head | Revenue (12 guests) | Cost | **Your profit** |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₹5,499 | 65,988 | 59,800 | **≈ ₹6,200** |

With early bird (first 4 seats at ₹5,499 = ₹1,200 less) → profit **≈ ₹8,600**.

**Final price: ₹5,799 per head (early bird ₹5,499).** Ooty is a more common/cheaper trip than Kodaikanal in Bangalore market (₹4,500–7,000), so it stays ₹500 below your Kodaikanal price.

## Break-even
Each missing guest saves only ~₹2,800 (stay + food + tickets).

| Paying guests | Approx. cost | Revenue at ₹5,799 | Profit / Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 59,800 | 69,588 | **+9,800** |
| 11 | 57,000 | 63,789 | **+6,800** |
| 10 | 54,200 | 57,990 | **+3,800** |
| 9 | 51,400 | 52,191 | **+800** |
| 8 | 48,600 | 46,392 | **–2,200 (loss)** |

→ **Minimum 10 bookings** before you confirm the TT.

## What's NOT included (tell guests clearly)
- Sunday dinner at Mysore (or include at +₹150)
- Toy train, Avalanche Lake jeep safari, horse riding, camera fees, personal shopping
- Extra costs due to landslides / road closure / forest gate delay

## Payment & cancellation policy (same as Kodaikanal)
- **Advance ₹2,000 per head (non-refundable)**. Balance 5 days before.
- Cancel 7+ days before: advance lost, balance refunded. Within 7 days: no refund (seat transfer allowed).
- Trip cancelled by you (red alert / landslide): full refund or shift date.
