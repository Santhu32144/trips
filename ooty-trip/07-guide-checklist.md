# Trip Guide Checklist – Ooty

## 3–4 weeks before
- [ ] Get **2–3 TT quotes**. Ask every owner:
  - 12+1 seater? Push-back seats? Luggage carrier?
  - ₹18/km – **minimum km per day?** (Ooty trip is short – this matters)
  - **Tolls, parking, TN permit, Nilgiris green tax – included or extra?**
  - Driver bata ₹500/day × 3 days – agreed?
  - AITP, insurance, fitness certificate, FASTag valid?
  - Driver has done **Bandipur–Mudumalai–Gudalur–Ooty** before? Knows the forest gate timings?
  - Get the deal **in writing on WhatsApp**.
- [ ] Shortlist 2–3 stays (TT parking + bonfire + driver room).
- [ ] Poster + WhatsApp message → start bookings.
- [ ] (Optional) If group wants **toy train**, book on IRCTC the day bookings open.

## 2 weeks before
- [ ] Minimum **9–10 bookings** reached? If not – postpone / smaller vehicle / adjust price.
- [ ] Pay TT & hotel advance. Save receipts.
- [ ] WhatsApp group – share itinerary, packing list, rules.
- [ ] Collect: full name, age, phone, emergency contact, ID copy, veg/non-veg, medical notes.

## 3–5 days before
- [ ] Collect balance.
- [ ] **Apply Ooty e-pass** (epass.tnega.org) – early, weekend limits. Share QR with driver.
- [ ] Confirm TN permit/border tax with TT owner.
- [ ] Re-confirm hotel & Gudalur breakfast hotel.
- [ ] Check weather + news for **landslides on Gudalur–Ooty road** and any **Bandipur/Mudumalai closure**.
- [ ] Share driver name, phone, vehicle number in the group.

## Trip day (Friday)
- [ ] Reach pickup 30 min early. Inspect TT (tyres, seat belts, cleanliness, spare tyre, wipers – rain!).
- [ ] Head count, ID check, read the rules.
- [ ] Make sure driver slept in the day.

## During the trip
- [ ] **Head count every time** the TT moves.
- [ ] Give exact return times at every stop.
- [ ] **5 key timings:** depart Fri 11 PM · forest gate 6 AM · checkout Sun 11 AM · **leave Ooty 1:30 PM** · cross Bandipur before 9 PM.
- [ ] Inside forest: windows can be open for photos from the seat, but **nobody gets down**, no shouting, no honking.
- [ ] Driver sleeps properly Saturday night. No alcohol for him.
- [ ] Note expenses immediately. Take group photos at every spot.

## Trip rules (read out in the TT)
1. Be on time – TT waits max 10 minutes. (On Sunday we **cannot** wait – forest gate closes 9 PM.)
2. No alcohol / smoking in the TT. Forest check posts check vehicles.
3. No getting down / littering / feeding animals in Bandipur & Mudumalai.
4. No plastic in Nilgiris.
5. Stay away from waterfall rocks and steep edges. No risky selfies.
6. Inform the guide before going anywhere alone.
7. Respect each other and the driver.

## After the trip
- [ ] Settle TT + driver bata (+ tip ₹200–500 if good).
- [ ] Share photos, ask for reviews.
- [ ] Update actual costs in [costing.csv](costing.csv).

## Emergency numbers
- **All emergencies: 112** · Ambulance **108** · Police **100** · Highway **1033**
- Forest emergency (wild animal on road): tell the forest check post staff / call 112
- Save: TT owner, driver, hotel, Ooty Government Hospital location, a backup taxi contact in Ooty.
