# Stay & Food

## Stay – 1 night (Saturday 31 Oct)

**What to look for**
- Group cottage / homestay / villa for **12 guests + guide (13 people)** – 3–4 rooms, 3–4 sharing, extra mattresses ok.
- **Bonfire allowed** – many Ooty cottages allow it (₹1,000–1,500 extra).
- **TT parking** – Ooty roads are narrow and steep. Ask "Can a 12-seater Tempo Traveller reach the gate?"
- **Hot water / geyser** – nights are 8–12°C.
- **Driver room/dorm** – he drives back the same day, he needs proper rest.
- **Check-in ~12:30 PM** Saturday is normal – no early check-in needed (we're sightseeing at Pykara in the morning).
- **Separate rooms for girls** in a mixed group.

**Good areas**
- **Near Ooty Lake / Fern Hill / Lovedale** – quiet cottages, easy to reach.
- **Near Charring Cross / Commercial Road** – walkable to market, but busy & less parking.
- **Doddabetta Road / Elk Hill** – views, close to Doddabetta for Sunday morning.

**How to book**
- Google Maps / Booking.com / MakeMyTrip / Airbnb: "group cottage Ooty", "homestay Ooty 12 people", "villa Ooty bonfire".
- Call directly for a group rate.
- Budget: **₹12,000–16,000 for the group per night** incl. bonfire (≈ ₹1,000–1,200 per head). Weekends cost more than weekdays.
- Pay 25–50% advance, get **written confirmation on WhatsApp**: dates, rooms, check-in time, bonfire, geyser, driver room, TT parking.

## Food plan (5 meals + night snacks)

| Meal | Where | Budget per head |
|---|---|---|
| Fri night tea/snacks | Mysore (snack pack in TT) | ₹50 |
| Sat breakfast | **Gudalur** – hotels on the main road (idli, dosa, pongal, Kerala-style puttu) | ₹120 |
| Sat lunch | Ooty town – meals / biryani | ₹250 |
| Sat dinner | At stay with bonfire (ask host to cook veg & non-veg) | ₹300 |
| Sun breakfast | At stay or town | ₹120 |
| Sun lunch | Ooty town before leaving | ₹250 |
| Sun dinner | **Mysore** – highway restaurant (guests' own, or add ₹150) | — |

**Tips**
- Call the Gudalur breakfast hotel the day before – 13 people at 7:30 AM is easy if they know.
- Ask guests at booking: **veg / non-veg / allergies**.
- Keep a 20 L water can in the TT; guests bring steel bottles (plastic ban).
- Must-try in Ooty: **homemade chocolate, varkey, hot tea, bread omelette** at the lake stalls.
