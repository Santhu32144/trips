# Packing List

Ooty in late October: **day 15–20°C, night 8–12°C**, rain + fog likely (NE monsoon).

## For guests (send on WhatsApp)

**Clothes**
- [ ] **Warm jacket / sweater** (must – Doddabetta at 7 AM is very cold)
- [ ] Cap / beanie, gloves, muffler
- [ ] 2 sets of comfortable clothes + night wear
- [ ] **Raincoat or umbrella** (must)
- [ ] Sports shoes with grip (Pykara, Pine Forest slopes are slippery)
- [ ] Slippers for stay
- [ ] Extra socks

**Documents**
- [ ] **Original ID proof** (Aadhaar / DL) – hotel check-in
- [ ] Cash + UPI (some places at Pykara/Shooting Point have no network)

**Personal**
- [ ] **Steel water bottle** – plastic is banned in Nilgiris
- [ ] Toiletries, towel
- [ ] Personal medicines
- [ ] Motion-sickness tablets (ghat road)
- [ ] Power bank + charger
- [ ] Neck pillow + shawl/blanket (we sleep in the TT at the forest gate)
- [ ] Moisturizer / lip balm, cold cream
- [ ] Small backpack

**Don't bring**
- Plastic bottles/covers, alcohol in the TT (forest check posts check vehicles), big suitcases

## Guide kit (YOU must carry)
- [ ] Printed + phone copy: guest list with phone & emergency contacts, TT permit, Ooty e-pass QR, hotel confirmation
- [ ] **First-aid kit:** band-aids, cotton, Betadine, Dolo 650, Avomine, ORS, Digene/Eno, Cetirizine, Volini spray, crepe bandage, Vicks, Strepsils, sanitizer
- [ ] **Cash ₹5,000** (green tax, parking, boating counters, emergency)
- [ ] Power bank (20,000 mAh) + multi-charger
- [ ] Torch / headlamp
- [ ] Whistle
- [ ] Garbage bags (no throwing anything in the forest!)
- [ ] Tissue rolls, wet wipes
- [ ] 20 L water can + steel/paper cups
- [ ] Night snack packs + biscuits for the forest-gate wait
- [ ] Bluetooth speaker, cards, dumb charades list for bonfire
- [ ] Notebook + pen (expenses)
- [ ] Spare umbrella / raincoat
- [ ] Tarpaulin + rope if luggage goes on the TT roof (rain!)
