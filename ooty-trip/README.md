# Ooty Trip – Bangalore (Trip Guide Plans)

Two versions of the Ooty weekend trip from Bangalore, same 12-seat Tempo Traveller.

| | [2day/](2day/) – 2 Days, 1 Night | [1day/](1day/) – 1 Day, No Stay |
|---|---|---|
| Dates | Fri 30 Oct night → Sun 1 Nov night | Fri 30 Oct night → Sun 1 Nov early morning |
| Stay | 1 night cottage + bonfire | No hotel – full day in Ooty |
| Going | Via Mysore – Bandipur – Gudalur – Pykara (forest gate 6 AM) | Same |
| Coming back | Leave Ooty Sun 1:30 PM, same road (forest closes 9 PM) | Leave Ooty Sat 9 PM via Coonoor – Mettupalayam – Salem – Hosur (no forest gate) |
| Back in Bangalore | Sun ~10:30 PM | Sun ~6:00 AM |
| Drivers | 1 | 2 (two nights of driving) |
| Meals | 5 | 3 + tea |
| Price per head | ₹5,799 (early bird ₹5,499) | ₹3,999 (early bird ₹3,799) |
| Advance | ₹2,000 | ₹1,000 |
| Guide profit (12 guests) | ~₹9,800 | ~₹6,700 |
| Break-even | 10 guests | 10 guests |
| Brochure | [Ooty-2-Day-Trip.pdf](2day/Ooty-2-Day-Trip.pdf) | [Ooty-1-Day-Trip.pdf](1day/Ooty-1-Day-Trip.pdf) |

## Other folders
- [image/](image/) – Ooty photos used in both brochures.
- [brochure/](brochure/) – brochure generator. Change prices, dates, text or photos in `build_brochure.py`, then run:

  ```
  python ooty-trip/brochure/build_brochure.py
  ```

  It rebuilds both PDFs and also saves a copy of each in `I:\trips\trip-pdf\` (all trip brochures together). Needs Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome, plus Pillow and pypdf. Fonts are in `brochure/fonts/` (free OFL licence).
