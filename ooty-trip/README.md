## Quick summary

- **Recommended selling price:** ₹5,799 per head (early bird ₹5,499) – all-inclusive: travel, stay, 5 meals, entry fees, boating, bonfire
- **Your cost per head (12 paying guests):** ~₹4,985
- **Expected guide profit:** ~₹9,800 for a full TT (~₹8,600 with 4 early-bird seats)
- **Break-even:** at least **10 paying guests**
- Ooty is **cheaper on travel** than Kodaikanal (shorter distance) but **costlier on stay and tickets**.

## Most important things (don't skip)

1. **Bandipur & Mudumalai forest roads are CLOSED 9 PM – 6 AM.** That's why we leave Bangalore at **11 PM** Friday (not 9:30 PM) and must **cross the forest before 9 PM on Sunday** – leave Ooty by **1:30 PM**.
2. **Ooty e-pass** for the TT – register on `epass.tnega.org` before the trip (weekend vehicle limits may apply – apply early).
3. **TN permit / border tax: ₹2,500** (confirmed) – get the receipt on WhatsApp before departure.
4. **Kalhatti ghat (36 hairpins via Masinagudi)** – very steep, has restrictions for outside/tourist vehicles. **We use the safer Gudalur – Pykara road** both ways.
5. **TT minimum km/day:** this trip is only ~700 km. If the TT owner has a 250 km/day minimum, you'll pay for 750 km. Confirm.
6. **No stopping, no getting down, no honking inside Bandipur/Mudumalai** – wild elephants. Fines are heavy.
