# Places & Photo Spots

Entry fees are approximate (Ooty raised several fees in recent years). Keep **₹600 per head** budget for all tickets + one boating.

## Main places (in the plan)

| Place | What it is | Time needed | Approx. fee | Best time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bandipur & Mudumalai drive** | Drive through two tiger reserves – elephants, deer, gaur, peacocks | 45 min (transit) | Free (no stopping) | 6 AM gate opening |
| **Pykara Falls** | Waterfall in shola forest, short walk | 30–40 min | ~₹20–50 | Morning |
| **Pykara Lake / Boat House** | Big forest lake, motor/speed boat rides | 45–60 min | Boat ~₹300–700 per person (type-wise) | Morning |
| **9th Mile Shooting Point** | Rolling green grasslands, many film shootings, horse ride | 30–45 min | ~₹10–30 | Morning |
| **Pine Forest** | Pine trees on a slope down to Kamaraj Sagar lake | 30–45 min | ~₹10–30 | Late morning / mist |
| **Government Botanical Garden** | 55-acre garden since 1848, fossil tree, glass house | 1–1.5 hr | ~₹50–100 | Afternoon |
| **Ooty Lake** | Boating (pedal / row / motor), mini train, horse ride | 1–1.5 hr | Boat ~₹300–600 per boat | Evening |
| **Charring Cross / Ooty Market** | Shopping – chocolates, tea, woollens, spices | 1 hr | — | Evening |
| **Doddabetta Peak** | Highest peak of Nilgiris, 2,637 m, viewpoint + telescope | 1 hr | ~₹10–30 + parking | Early morning |
| **Tea Factory & Tea Museum** | Tea-making process, tasting, fresh tea shop | 45 min | ~₹10–20 | Morning |
| **Rose Garden** | Largest rose garden in India, terraced | 45 min | ~₹50–100 | Late morning |

## Top photo spots 📸
1. **Pine Forest** – group shot between the trees with lake in the background.
2. **9th Mile Shooting Point** – green hills, "Bollywood song" shots.
3. **Doddabetta Peak** – above-the-clouds sunrise.
4. **Pykara Lake** – speed boat ride videos.
5. **Botanical Garden** – Italian garden & glass house.
6. **Rose Garden** – colourful terraces.
7. **Tea estates** on the way to Doddabetta – stop safely on the roadside for a photo in the tea rows.
8. **Ooty Lake** – evening boating & sunset.

## Shopping
- **Homemade chocolates** (Ooty is famous – shops at Charring Cross & Commercial Road) – ₹100–150 per 100 g
- **Ooty varkey** (crispy local biscuit) and bakery items
- **Nilgiri tea** – buy fresh at the Tea Factory
- **Eucalyptus oil, spices**
- **Woollens** – Tibetan market near the lake

## Rules at Ooty
- **Plastic ban in Nilgiris** – no plastic bottles below 2 L / carry bags. Carry steel bottles. Checked at entry.
- Inside Bandipur & Mudumalai – no stopping, getting down, honking or feeding animals.
- Don't feed monkeys at Doddabetta & Botanical Garden.
- Pykara Falls – stay on the path, rocks are slippery.
