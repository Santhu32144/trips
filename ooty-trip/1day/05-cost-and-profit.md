# Cost & Profit Calculation – 1-Day Plan (No Stay)

## Assumptions
- TT with **12 passenger seats + 2 drivers. All 12 seats are for paying guests.** You (guide) sit in front next to the driver.
- TT rate **₹18/km** (includes TT rent + Driver 1 salary). Tolls, parking, permit = extra (confirm!).
- **2nd driver ₹2,000** for the trip (salary + bata) – confirm with the owner.
- Driver 1 bata **₹500/day × 2 days**.
- Actual distance: ~305 (going) + ~430 (coming back via Salem) + ~25 local ≈ **760 km**. **Billed: 780 km** (buffer). The usual 250 km/day × 3 days = 750 km minimum doesn't matter here – we're above it.
- Package is **all-inclusive**: travel, 3 meals + tea, entry tickets, Pykara boating. **No stay.**
- Food and tickets for **13 people** (12 guests + you). Drivers' food is covered by bata.

## Selling price & your profit (12 paying guests)

| Price per head | Revenue (12 guests) | Cost | **Your profit** |
|---|---|---|---|
| ₹3,799 | 45,588 | 41,290 | **≈ ₹4,300** |
| **₹3,999 (final price)** | 47,988 | 41,290 | **≈ ₹6,700** |
| ₹4,199 | 50,388 | 41,290 | **≈ ₹9,100** |

With early bird (first 4 seats at ₹3,799 = ₹800 less) → profit **≈ ₹5,900**.

**Final price: ₹3,999 per head (early bird ₹3,799).** Staying under ₹4,000 makes it an easy "1-day Ooty" sell. If you want the same ~₹9,000 profit as the 2-day trip, price it at ₹4,199.

**Why it can't be much cheaper:** transport (₹24,040) is ~60% of the cost and is *higher* than the 2-day trip (2nd driver, rest room, longer road back, more tolls). You only save the stay, bonfire and 2 meals.

## Break-even
Each missing guest saves only ~₹1,250 (food + tickets).

| Paying guests | Approx. cost | Revenue at ₹3,999 | Profit / Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 41,290 | 47,988 | **+6,700** |
| 11 | 40,040 | 43,989 | **+3,950** |
| 10 | 38,790 | 39,990 | **+1,200** |
| 9 | 37,540 | 35,991 | **–1,550 (loss)** |
| 8 | 36,290 | 31,992 | **–4,300 (loss)** |

→ **Minimum 10 bookings** before you confirm the TT.

## What's NOT included (tell guests clearly)
- **Any hotel room** – this is a no-stay trip (freshen up at Gudalur)
- Friday dinner (eat before reporting) and Sunday breakfast
- Ooty Lake boating, toy train, horse riding, camera fees, personal shopping
- Extra costs due to landslides / road closure / forest gate delay

## Payment & cancellation policy (same as the 2-day trip, smaller advance)
- **Advance ₹1,000 per head (non-refundable)**. Balance 5 days before.
- Cancel 7+ days before: advance lost, balance refunded. Within 7 days: no refund (seat transfer allowed).
- Trip cancelled by you (red alert / landslide): full refund or shift date.
