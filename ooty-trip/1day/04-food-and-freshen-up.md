# Food, Freshen-up & Driver Rest (No Stay)

## No hotel – what guests must know
- There is **no room for guests** on this trip. We freshen up at the **Gudalur breakfast hotel** (restrooms) on Saturday at 7:30 AM.
- Tell guests at booking: carry a **small towel, toothbrush, wet wipes, deodorant and a fresh T-shirt + socks** to change at Gudalur.
- **Optional:** book 1–2 rooms near Gudalur for an hour (~₹800–1,000 each) so guests can wash and change in turns. Not in the package price – add only if the group asks.
- No hotel means no charging – guests carry **power banks**; check the TT has USB charging points.

## Driver rest room (MUST book)
- Book a basic room in Ooty (near Charring Cross / bus stand) **from 1 PM to 8:30 PM** for **Driver 1** – ~₹600–800.
- Driver 1 drives Bangalore → Ooty, sleeps here in the afternoon, then drives the last stretch **Salem → Bangalore** (2 AM – 6 AM).
- **Driver 2** sleeps in the TT on Friday night, drives Ooty local in the afternoon, rests during dinner, then drives **Ooty → Salem** (9 PM – 1:45 AM).
- Never let one driver do both nights. If the owner sends only one driver, use **Plan B** in [01-route-and-permit.md](01-route-and-permit.md).

| Sat evening tea + snack | Tea Factory / Doddabetta stalls | ₹50 |

| **Total** | | **≈ ₹750** |

**Tips**
- Call the Gudalur breakfast hotel, the Ooty lunch place and the dinner place the day before – 13 people + 2 drivers.
- **Book dinner for 7:45 PM sharp** – Ooty restaurants are packed on Saturday night and we must leave by 9 PM.
- Keep dinner light – the Coonoor ghat (14 hairpins) comes right after dinner.
- Ask guests at booking: **veg / non-veg / allergies**.
- Keep a 20 L water can in the TT; guests bring steel bottles (plastic ban).
- Must-try in Ooty: **homemade chocolate, varkey, hot tea, bread omelette**.
