# Ooty 1-Day Trip (No Stay) – Bangalore (Trip Guide Plan)

**Trip type:** Group trip, 12 seats, Tempo Traveller (TT) with **2 drivers**, **no hotel stay**
**Dates:** Friday 30 Oct night → Sunday 1 Nov early morning (full day in Ooty: Sat 31 Oct)
**Route:** A loop – **go** via Mysore – Bandipur – Mudumalai – Gudalur – Pykara (forest drive at sunrise), **come back** at night via Coonoor – Mettupalayam – Salem – Krishnagiri – Hosur (no forest gate)
**States crossed:** Only Karnataka ↔ Tamil Nadu. No Kerala.
**Distance:** ~305 km going + ~430 km coming back + ~25 km local ≈ **760 km**

## Quick summary

- **Recommended selling price:** ₹3,999 per head (early bird ₹3,799) – travel, 3 meals + tea, all entry tickets, Pykara boating
- **Your cost per head (12 paying guests):** ~₹3,440
- **Expected guide profit:** ~₹6,700 for a full TT (~₹5,900 with 4 early-bird seats)
- **Break-even:** at least **10 paying guests**
- Transport costs **more than the 2-day trip** (same km + 2nd driver + longer road back). The only savings are the stay, bonfire and 2 meals – that's why the price can't drop much below ₹4,000.

## Most important things (don't skip)

1. **Leave Ooty at 9:00 PM Saturday via Coonoor – Mettupalayam.** The Bandipur & Mudumalai forest road is **closed 9 PM – 6 AM**, so the night return must go via Salem – Hosur (highway, open all night).
2. **2 drivers – mandatory.** The TT runs two nights back-to-back (~760 km in ~31 hrs). One driver alone is not safe. Driver 1 sleeps in a day-rest room in Ooty (1 PM – 8:30 PM) and drives the last stretch; Driver 2 drives Ooty local + Ooty → Salem.
3. **Forest gate 6 AM Saturday** – leave Bangalore 11 PM Friday and wait at the Melkamanahalli gate, same as the 2-day trip.
4. **Ooty e-pass** for the TT – apply on `epass.tnega.org` early (weekend vehicle limits). It's a day visit – no stay address.
5. **TN permit / border tax: ₹2,500** – one receipt covers the whole loop (enter TN at Kakkanahalla, leave at Hosur).
6. **No hotel** – guests freshen up at the Gudalur breakfast hotel. Tell them to carry a small towel, toothbrush and a fresh T-shirt.
7. **Saturday is packed.** If you're running late, drop **Rose Garden** first, then the **Tea Factory** (see [02-itinerary.md](02-itinerary.md)).
8. **Drop is ~6 AM Sunday on the Hosur Road side** (Electronic City / Silk Board). Tell guests at booking so they plan their ride home.
