# Itinerary – Friday 30 Oct Night to Sunday 1 Nov Morning (No Stay)

## FRIDAY – Departure night

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 10:15 PM | Guide reaches pickup point. Check TT: **both drivers present**, seats clean, FASTag balance, permit, e-pass QR, first-aid kit, water can, charging points |
| 10:30 PM | Guest reporting time – **one** pickup point (e.g., Silk Board / or Mysore Road side like Kengeri / Nayandahalli – closer to the expressway). Remind everyone: **drop is ~6 AM Sunday on the Hosur Road side** |
| **11:00 PM** | **Depart** (Driver 1 drives, Driver 2 sleeps) |
| 11:15 PM | Intro round, trip rules, emergency numbers, WhatsApp group check |
| 1:30 AM | Tea break – Mysore |
| ~3:15 AM | Reach Melkamanahalli forest gate (Bandipur). Park in queue. **Everyone sleeps** |

## SATURDAY – Full day in Ooty (forest drive + Pykara side + Doddabetta side + town)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| **6:00 AM** | Forest gate opens. Drive through **Bandipur & Mudumalai** – best time for elephants, spotted deer, peacocks, gaur 🐘 (no stopping!) |
| 6:30 AM | Kakkanahalla TN check post – permit + e-pass check |
| 7:30 AM | **Gudalur** – freshen up (hotel restrooms), breakfast. Give motion-sickness tablets |
| 8:15 AM | Leave Gudalur |
| 9:00 AM | **Pykara Falls** – short walk, waterfall in the forest (40 min) |
| 9:50 AM | **Pykara Lake / Boat House** – motor / speed boat (1 hr). Guide goes straight to the ticket counter – weekend queues |
| 11:05 AM | **9th Mile Shooting Point** – rolling green meadows, film shooting spot (35 min) |
| 11:50 AM | **Pine Forest** – tall pines down to Kamaraj Sagar lake – best group photos (40 min) |
| 12:45 PM | Reach Ooty – **lunch** at Charring Cross. **Driver 1 checks in to the rest room and sleeps till 8:30 PM.** Driver 2 takes over |
| 2:00 PM | **Doddabetta Peak** (~10 km) – highest point in the Nilgiris (2,637 m), viewpoint + telescope house (45 min) |
| 3:00 PM | **Tea Factory & Tea Museum** (Doddabetta road) – how tea is made, tasting, buy fresh tea. Chocolate factory next door (45 min) |
| 4:00 PM | **Government Botanical Garden** – 150-year-old garden, fossil tree, Italian garden, glass house (1 hr 15 min) |
| 5:30 PM | **Rose Garden** – terraced rose garden (40 min – closes ~6:30 PM) |
| 6:30 PM | **Charring Cross / Ooty Market** – homemade chocolates, varkey, tea powder, eucalyptus oil, woollens |
| 7:45 PM | **Dinner** in town (pre-booked). Driver 2 eats and rests |
| 8:30 PM | Pick up Driver 1 from the rest room. Head count, luggage check. Motion-sickness tablets for the ghat |
| **9:00 PM** | **DEPART Ooty** via Coonoor – Mettupalayam (Driver 2 drives) |

| 10:45 PM | Mettupalayam – restroom / tea |
| ~1:45 AM | **Salem** – tea break. **Driver change** – Driver 1 drives to Bangalore |

## Running late? Drop in this order

1. **Rose Garden** – skip, or see it only from the gate.
2. **Tea Factory** – stop at a roadside tea shop on the Doddabetta road instead.
3. **9th Mile Shooting Point** – 10-minute photo stop only.

Never move the **6 AM forest gate** or the **9 PM Ooty departure** – everything else bends around those two.

## Optional / alternate spots
- **Ooty Lake boating** instead of Rose Garden – boating closes ~6 PM, long weekend queues.
- **Avalanche Lake & Emerald Lake** – needs 3–4 hrs. Not possible in one day.
- **Nilgiri Mountain Railway (toy train)** – not possible in this plan.
- **Coonoor** (Sim's Park, Dolphin's Nose) – closes by evening; we only pass through Coonoor at night.
- **Thread Garden / Wax Museum** – small, in town, good for the rain plan.

## Rain plan
- Doddabetta can be fully in fog – still go (it's on the way to the Tea Factory). If zero visibility, spend more time at the Tea Museum + Chocolate factory.
- Pykara Falls & Pine Forest are slippery in rain – hold the group, no going near the falls rocks.
- Heavy rain at night makes the Coonoor ghat foggy – **leave at 8:30 PM** and drive slow.
- **Landslide alert on the Coonoor – Mettupalayam road?** Use Plan B – wait at Gudalur, cross Bandipur at 6 AM, reach Bangalore by Sunday noon (see [01-route-and-permit.md](01-route-and-permit.md)). Tell guests early.
