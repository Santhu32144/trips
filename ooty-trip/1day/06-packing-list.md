# Packing List – 1-Day Trip (No Stay)

Ooty in late October: **day 15–20°C, night 8–12°C**, rain + fog likely (NE monsoon).
No hotel – guests are in the TT for **two nights** (Fri & Sat) and out all day Saturday. Pack in **one small backpack**.

## For guests (send on WhatsApp)

**Clothes**
- [ ] **Warm jacket / sweater** (must – Doddabetta and the night drive are cold)
- [ ] Cap / beanie, muffler
- [ ] **Raincoat or umbrella** (must)
- [ ] Sports shoes with grip (Pykara, Pine Forest slopes are slippery)
- [ ] **1 fresh T-shirt + extra socks** – to change at Gudalur on Saturday morning

**Freshen-up kit** (no hotel – we freshen up at the Gudalur breakfast hotel)
- [ ] Small towel, toothbrush + paste
- [ ] Wet wipes, deodorant, face wash
- [ ] Moisturizer / lip balm

**Documents & money**
- [ ] **Original ID proof** (Aadhaar / DL)
- [ ] Cash + UPI (no network at Pykara / Shooting Point / in the forest)

**For the TT nights**
- [ ] Neck pillow + shawl / light blanket
- [ ] Eye mask / earplugs
- [ ] **Power bank (fully charged) + cable** – no hotel to charge phones

**Personal**
- [ ] **Steel water bottle** – plastic is banned in the Nilgiris
- [ ] Personal medicines
- [ ] Motion-sickness tablets (Gudalur ghat going, Coonoor ghat coming back)
- [ ] Snacks / dry fruits

**Don't bring**
- Plastic bottles/covers, alcohol (forest check posts check vehicles), big bags or suitcases

## Guide kit (YOU must carry)
- [ ] Printed + phone copy: guest list with phone & emergency contacts, TT permit, Ooty e-pass QR, **both drivers' names & numbers**, rest room booking, dinner booking
- [ ] **First-aid kit:** band-aids, cotton, Betadine, Dolo 650, Avomine, ORS, Digene/Eno, Cetirizine, Volini spray, crepe bandage, Vicks, Strepsils, sanitizer
- [ ] **Cash ₹5,000** (green tax, parking, boating counter, rest room, emergency)
- [ ] Power bank (20,000 mAh) + multi-charger
- [ ] Torch / headlamp
- [ ] Whistle
- [ ] Garbage bags (no throwing anything in the forest!)
- [ ] Tissue rolls, wet wipes
- [ ] 20 L water can + steel/paper cups
- [ ] Night snack packs + biscuits for the forest-gate wait and the night drive
- [ ] Notebook + pen (expenses)
- [ ] Spare umbrella / raincoat
- [ ] Thermos of tea/coffee for the front seat (you stay awake with the driver through the Coonoor ghat)
