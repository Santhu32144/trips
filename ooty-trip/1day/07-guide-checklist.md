# Trip Guide Checklist – Ooty 1-Day (No Stay)

## 3–4 weeks before
- [ ] Get **2–3 TT quotes**. Ask every owner:
  - 12+1 seater? Push-back seats? **USB charging points?** (guests spend two nights in the TT)
  - **2 drivers for this trip? What does the 2nd driver cost?** (must – see below)
  - ₹18/km – minimum km per day? (this loop is ~760 km)
  - **Tolls, parking, TN permit, Nilgiris green tax – included or extra?**
  - AITP, insurance, fitness certificate, FASTag valid?
  - Drivers know **Bandipur – Mudumalai – Gudalur – Ooty** (forest gate timings) AND the **Coonoor – Mettupalayam ghat at night**?
  - Get the deal **in writing on WhatsApp**.
- [ ] Shortlist: Gudalur breakfast hotel, Ooty lunch + dinner places, **driver rest room** in Ooty.
- [ ] Poster + WhatsApp message → start bookings.

## 2 weeks before
- [ ] Minimum **10 bookings** reached? If not – postpone / switch to Plan B (1 driver, back Sunday noon) / adjust price.
- [ ] Pay TT advance. Book the driver rest room. Save receipts.
- [ ] WhatsApp group – share itinerary, packing list, rules. Say clearly: **no hotel** and **drop ~6 AM Sunday on the Hosur Road side**.
- [ ] Collect: full name, age, phone, emergency contact, ID copy, veg/non-veg, medical notes.

## 3–5 days before
- [ ] Collect balance.
- [ ] **Apply Ooty e-pass** (epass.tnega.org) – day visit. Share the QR with both drivers.
- [ ] Confirm TN permit/border tax with the TT owner.
- [ ] Re-confirm: **both drivers' names & phone numbers**, rest room, Gudalur breakfast, Ooty lunch, **Ooty dinner at 7:45 PM**.
- [ ] Check weather + news: landslides on **Gudalur – Ooty** and **Coonoor – Mettupalayam** roads, any Bandipur/Mudumalai closure.
- [ ] Share driver names, phone numbers and vehicle number in the group.

## Trip day (Friday)
- [ ] Reach pickup 30 min early. Inspect TT (tyres, seat belts, wipers, spare tyre, charging points).
- [ ] **Both drivers present.** Driver 1 slept in the day. Driver 2 will sleep in the TT tonight.
- [ ] Head count, ID check, read the rules.

## During the trip
- [ ] **Head count every time** the TT moves.
- [ ] Give exact return times at every stop.
- [ ] **6 key timings:** depart Fri 11 PM · forest gate 6 AM · Ooty lunch 12:45 PM · Driver 1 in the rest room by 1 PM · **leave Ooty 9 PM** · Bangalore ~6 AM.
- [ ] Running late? Drop Rose Garden → Tea Factory → Shooting Point (in that order). Never cut the 9 PM departure.
- [ ] Inside the forest: windows can be open for photos from the seat, but **nobody gets down**, no shouting, no honking.
- [ ] **Coonoor ghat at night:** sit in front, stay awake, keep the driver talking. If he looks sleepy – stop at Mettupalayam for tea, no arguments.
- [ ] **Driver change at Salem.** No alcohol for either driver.
- [ ] Note expenses immediately. Take group photos at every spot.

## Trip rules (read out in the TT)
1. Be on time – TT waits max 10 minutes. Saturday is packed – if one stop runs late, we drop the last place, not the departure time.
2. No alcohol / smoking in the TT. Forest check posts check vehicles.
3. No getting down / littering / feeding animals in Bandipur & Mudumalai.
4. No plastic in the Nilgiris.
5. Stay away from waterfall rocks and steep edges. No risky selfies.
6. Inform the guide before going anywhere alone.
7. Let the drivers sleep when it's their turn – keep the noise down at night.
8. Respect each other and the drivers.

## After the trip
- [ ] At the drop (~6 AM): make sure **every guest has a ride home** – especially anyone travelling alone.
- [ ] Settle TT + both drivers' bata (+ tip ₹200–500 each if good).
- [ ] Share photos, ask for reviews.
- [ ] Update actual costs in [costing.csv](costing.csv).

## Emergency numbers
- **All emergencies: 112** · Ambulance **108** · Police **100** · Highway **1033**
- Forest emergency (wild animal on road): tell the forest check post staff / call 112
- Save: TT owner, both drivers, rest room, dinner restaurant, Ooty Government Hospital location, a backup taxi contact in Ooty.
