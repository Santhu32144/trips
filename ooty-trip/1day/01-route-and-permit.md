# Route & Permit – 1-Day Loop

## The loop (Karnataka ↔ Tamil Nadu only)

**Going – Fri night → Sat morning (~305 km, same as the 2-day trip)**
Bangalore → Mysore (Expressway) → Nanjangud → Gundlupet → **Bandipur (KA) → Mudumalai (TN)** → Gudalur → Naduvattam → Pykara → Ooty

**Coming back – Sat night → Sun morning (~430 km)**
Ooty → Coonoor → **Mettupalayam** → Annur → Avinashi → (NH 544) Perundurai → Salem → (NH 44) Dharmapuri → Krishnagiri → Hosur → Bangalore

Only one state border both ways – Karnataka / Tamil Nadu (Kakkanahalla going, Hosur–Attibele coming back). No Kerala.

### Why a different road back?
- **Bandipur & Mudumalai are closed 9 PM – 6 AM.** If we stay in Ooty till night, we can't go back the way we came.
- Waiting at the forest gate till 6 AM (Plan B below) means reaching Bangalore only around **Sunday noon**.
- The Coonoor – Mettupalayam – Salem – Hosur road has **no forest gate**. It's 4-lane highway from Avinashi, with fuel and food open all night. We reach Bangalore by **~6 AM Sunday**.

### Night-driving notes (coming back)
- **Coonoor – Mettupalayam ghat: 14 hairpin bends, downhill, often foggy at night.** Low gear, no overtaking, slow. Give motion-sickness tablets after dinner, before leaving Ooty.
- **Kallar / Burliyar stretch** (bottom of the ghat) – elephants can cross at night. Don't stop, don't get down.
- **Don't take the Sathyamangalam – Dhimbam ghat** from Mettupalayam, even if Google Maps shows it as shorter. It goes through Sathyamangalam Tiger Reserve with 27 hairpin bends – not a night drive with guests.
- **Don't take Kalhatti ghat** (Ooty → Masinagudi) – restricted for tourist vehicles, and it leads back to the closed forest road anyway.
- **Driver change at Salem** (~1:45 AM). Driver 1 slept in the afternoon, so he takes the last stretch to Bangalore.
- The guide sits in front and stays awake through the ghat – keep the driver talking.

## Plan B – if you can't get 2 drivers (or the Coonoor road is closed)

Come back the same way you went, and **wait before the forest**:

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 9:00 PM | Leave Ooty via Naduvattam |
| 10:30 PM | **Gudalur** – park at a fuel station / hotel. Everyone sleeps in the TT; driver gets ~6.5 hrs sleep |
| 5:15 AM | Leave Gudalur |
| 6:00 AM | Mudumalai / Bandipur gates open – forest drive (morning wildlife 🐘) |
| 8:30 AM | Mysore – breakfast (guests' own, or add ₹120) |
| **11:30 AM – 12 PM** | **Reach Bangalore** (Mysore Road side) |

- ~₹3,300 cheaper than the Salem return (no 2nd driver, no rest room, fewer tolls) – see [05-cost-and-profit.md](05-cost-and-profit.md).
- But guests spend a second night in the TT and reach only by Sunday noon. **If you sell Plan B, say "back by Sunday noon" on the poster.**
- Wait in **Gudalur**, not at the forest check post – Gudalur has restrooms, tea shops and fuel stations.

## Permit / border tax
- Same as the 2-day trip: **All India Tourist Permit (AITP)** or **TN border/entry tax ₹2,500** (Vahan / check post). Receipt on WhatsApp before departure.
- We enter Tamil Nadu at Kakkanahalla and leave at Hosur – **no extra tax** for leaving TN or re-entering Karnataka.

## Ooty (Nilgiris) e-pass
- Needed for the TT – **https://epass.tnega.org**. Apply as early as allowed for weekends (vehicle limits).
- This is a **day visit** – if the form asks for stay details, choose the day-visit option.
- Checked when entering the Nilgiris on the Gudalur side. Nothing needed to leave via Mettupalayam.
- Save the QR on your phone AND both drivers' phones.

## Tolls & other charges
- **Tolls:** Bangalore–Mysore Expressway going (~₹500–600 for a TT) + NH 544 (Avinashi – Salem) and NH 44 (Salem – Krishnagiri – Attibele) coming back. Budget **~₹2,500** for the loop. Keep FASTag balance above ₹3,000.
- **Nilgiris green tax / entry fee** at the check post (~₹100–300 for a TT).
- **Parking** at Pykara, Shooting Point, Pine Forest, Doddabetta, Botanical Garden, Rose Garden: ₹50–150 each.
