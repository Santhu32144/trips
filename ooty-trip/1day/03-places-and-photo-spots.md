# Places & Photo Spots – 1-Day Plan

Entry fees and timings are approximate (Ooty changes fees often – check a week before). Keep **₹500 per head** budget for all tickets + Pykara boating.

## Places in the plan

| Place | What it is | Time needed | Approx. fee | Open (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bandipur & Mudumalai drive** | Drive through two tiger reserves – elephants, deer, gaur, peacocks | 45 min (transit) | Free (no stopping) | Gate opens 6 AM |
| **Pykara Falls** | Waterfall in shola forest, short walk | 40 min | ~₹20–50 | Morning |
| **Pykara Lake / Boat House** | Big forest lake, motor/speed boat rides | 1 hr | Motor boat ~₹750 per boat (up to 8); speed boat extra | ~9 AM – 5 PM |
| **9th Mile Shooting Point** | Rolling green grasslands, film shooting spot | 35 min | ~₹10–30 | Day time |
| **Pine Forest** | Pine trees on a slope down to Kamaraj Sagar lake | 40 min | ~₹10–30 | Day time |
| **Doddabetta Peak** | Highest peak of the Nilgiris, 2,637 m, viewpoint + telescope | 45 min | ~₹10–30 + parking | ~7 AM – 6 PM |
| **Tea Factory & Tea Museum** | Tea-making process, tasting, fresh tea shop | 45 min | ~₹10–20 | Day time |
| **Government Botanical Garden** | 55-acre garden since 1848, fossil tree, glass house | 1 hr 15 min | ~₹30–100 | ~7 AM – 6:30 PM |
| **Rose Garden** | Terraced rose garden | 40 min | ~₹30–100 | ~7:30 AM – 6:30 PM |
| **Charring Cross / Ooty Market** | Shopping – chocolates, tea, woollens, spices | 1 hr | — | Evening |

**Not in the 1-day plan:** Ooty Lake boating, Avalanche Lake, toy train, Coonoor spots (all in the "optional" list in [02-itinerary.md](02-itinerary.md)).

## Top photo spots 📸
1. **Pine Forest** – group shot between the trees with the lake behind.
2. **9th Mile Shooting Point** – green hills, "Bollywood song" shots.
3. **Pykara Lake** – speed boat ride videos.
4. **Doddabetta Peak** – view over the Nilgiris (afternoon – clouds may roll in, that's a photo too).
5. **Tea estates on the Doddabetta road** – stop safely on the roadside for a photo in the tea rows.
6. **Botanical Garden** – Italian garden & glass house.
7. **Rose Garden** – colourful terraces.
8. **Bandipur at 6 AM** – wildlife from the TT window (no stopping!).

## Shopping (Charring Cross, 6:30 PM)
- **Homemade chocolates** – ₹100–150 per 100 g
- **Ooty varkey** (crispy local biscuit) and bakery items
- **Nilgiri tea** – buy fresh at the Tea Factory (3 PM) – cheaper than town
- **Eucalyptus oil, spices**
- **Woollens** – shops around Charring Cross / Commercial Road

## Rules at Ooty
- **Plastic ban in the Nilgiris** – no plastic bottles below 2 L / carry bags. Carry steel bottles. Checked at entry.
- Inside Bandipur & Mudumalai – no stopping, getting down, honking or feeding animals.
- Don't feed monkeys at Doddabetta & Botanical Garden.
- Pykara Falls – stay on the path, rocks are slippery.
