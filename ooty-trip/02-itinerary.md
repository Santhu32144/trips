## Optional / alternate spots
- **Avalanche Lake & Emerald Lake** (~25 km) – forest dept eco-tourism jeep ride, very scenic. Needs 3–4 hrs → swap with Botanical Garden + Lake if group prefers nature.
- **Nilgiri Mountain Railway (toy train)** Ooty → Coonoor – UNESCO heritage. Tickets sell out weeks ahead on IRCTC; return by road is extra time. Only if booked early.
- **Coonoor** – Sim's Park, Lamb's Rock, Dolphin's Nose (Coonoor), tea estates. Opposite direction – fits only in a 3-day plan.
- **Thread Garden / Wax Museum / Deer Park** – small, in town, good for rain plan.
- **Kamaraj Sagar Dam** – on the Pykara road, sunset spot.
- **Kalhatti Falls** – only on the Kalhatti route (not on our route).

## Rain plan
- Doddabetta can be fully in fog – still go; if zero visibility, do Tea Museum + Chocolate factory instead.
- Indoor: Tea Museum, Chocolate factory, Thread Garden, Wax Museum, cafés at Charring Cross.
- Pykara Falls & Pine Forest are slippery in rain – hold the group, no going near the falls rocks.
