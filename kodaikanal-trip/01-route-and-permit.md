# Route & Permit

## Best route (Karnataka → Tamil Nadu only)

**Bangalore → Hosur → Krishnagiri → Dharmapuri → Salem → Namakkal → Karur → Dindigul → Batlagundu → Kodaikanal**
(NH44 all the way to Dindigul, then Batlagundu ghat road)

You cross only **one state border – Attibele (KA) / Hosur (TN)**. After that, everything is in Tamil Nadu. One permit is enough.

| Stop | Approx. km from Bangalore (Silk Board) | Approx. time if leaving 9:30 PM |
|---|---|---|
| Electronic City / Attibele border | 25 | 10:15 PM |
| Hosur (TN check post) | 40 | 10:30 PM |
| Krishnagiri | 90 | 11:30 PM – tea break |
| Dharmapuri | 140 | 12:30 AM |
| Salem | 200 | 1:30 AM |
| Namakkal | 255 | 2:30 AM |
| Karur | 300 | 3:15 AM – tea / restroom break |
| Dindigul | 375 | 4:30 AM |
| Batlagundu (bottom of ghat) | 410 | 5:15 AM |
| Silver Cascade Falls (on the ghat) | 455 | 6:45 AM |
| Kodaikanal town | ~470 | 7:15–7:30 AM |

Total drive: **~9.5–10 hours** with 2–3 breaks.

### Why this route
- 4-lane highway till Dindigul – safest for night driving in a TT.
- Batlagundu ghat (Kodai Road) is wider and has easier bends than the Palani ghat – better for a TT.
- Only one border crossing.

### Routes to AVOID
- **Via Mysore – Sathyamangalam – Dimbam ghat:** forest road is **closed at night (approx. 6 PM – 6 AM)** and has 27 sharp hairpins. Not for a Friday-night TT.
- **Via Kerala (Palakkad / Munnar side):** extra state = extra permit. Not needed.
- **Palani ghat** (Palani → Kodai): narrower and steeper. Use only if you plan a Palani temple visit, and only in daylight.

## Ghat road notes
- Batlagundu → Kodaikanal is ~65 km uphill with many hairpin bends. Takes ~1.5–2 hrs.
- Climb at dawn (5:30 AM onwards) – that's why we plan to reach Batlagundu by ~5:15 AM.
- Oct–Dec is **North-East monsoon** – expect rain and heavy fog on the ghat. Tell the driver to go slow, fog lights on.
- Motion sickness is common – give tablets (Avomine) to guests **30 min before** the ghat starts (around Dindigul).
- No overtaking on hairpins. Uphill vehicles have right of way.

## Permit / border tax (IMPORTANT)
A Karnataka-registered tourist TT entering Tamil Nadu needs either:
1. **All India Tourist Permit (AITP)** – if the TT has this, usually no extra border tax; OR
2. **TN border / entry tax** paid online on **Vahan (vahan.parivahan.gov.in → "Border Tax / Other State Vehicle Tax")** or at the Hosur check post.

**Ask the TT owner clearly:**
- Does the TT have AITP?
- Is the TN tax included in ₹18/km, or extra? (Usually EXTRA and paid by customer.)
- Confirmed: **₹2,500**.
- Get the permit receipt on WhatsApp before departure.

## Kodaikanal e-pass (IMPORTANT)
- Since May 2024 (Madras High Court order), all vehicles entering **Kodaikanal and Ooty** need an **e-pass**.
- Apply at **https://epass.tnega.org** – need vehicle number, driver details, number of passengers, dates, stay address.
- Free, takes a few minutes. Do it 2–3 days before the trip. Save the QR code PDF on your phone AND driver's phone.
- Check the site a week before – rules (and any vehicle limit on weekends) can change.

## Other charges on the way
- **Tolls:** about 8–9 toll plazas each way on NH44. TT counts as LCV/mini-bus. Budget **~₹2,000 one way / ~₹4,000 round trip**. Make sure the TT has a working FASTag.
- **Kodaikanal entry / green tax:** small fee at the check post near Silver Cascade (~₹100–300 for a TT).
- **Parking fees** at lake, Coaker's Walk, forest points: ₹50–150 each time.
