## Quick summary

- **Recommended selling price:** ₹6,299 per head, early bird ₹5,999 (all-inclusive: travel, stay, 5 meals, entry fees, bonfire)
- **Your cost per head (12 paying guests):** ~₹5,175
- **Expected guide profit:** ~₹13,500 for a full TT (~₹12,300 with 4 early-bird seats) (12 guests; you sit in front with the driver)
- **Break-even:** you need at least **10 paying guests** at ₹6,299. Below that, you lose money.
- **Book 2–3 weeks ahead.** Collect advance before booking TT and stay.

## Most important things (don't skip)

1. **Kodaikanal e-pass** for the TT – register on `epass.tnega.org` before the trip (needed since 2024; check it's still in force).
2. **TN permit for the TT: ₹2,500** (confirmed) – get the receipt on WhatsApp before departure.
3. **Driver must sleep properly Saturday night** – he drives through 2 nights. Book him a bed.
4. **Plastic is banned in Kodaikanal** – no plastic bottles/covers, fines are charged.
5. **Leave Kodaikanal by 1:00 PM Sunday** to reach Bangalore by ~11:30 PM.

> Prices are estimates for Oct–Nov 2026. Call and confirm hotel, TT and toll amounts before you fix the final price.
