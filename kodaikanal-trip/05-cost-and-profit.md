# Cost & Profit Calculation

## Assumptions
- TT with **12 passenger seats + driver. All 12 seats are for paying guests.** You (guide) sit in the front seat next to the driver.
- TT rate **₹18/km** (includes TT rent + driver salary). Tolls, parking, permit = extra (confirm!).
- Driver bata **₹500/day × 3 days** (Fri, Sat, Sun).
- Total distance billed: **~1,050 km** (470 × 2 + ~60 km local + buffer).
- Package is **all-inclusive**: travel, stay, 5 meals, **entry** tickets, bonfire.
- Stay, food and tickets are for **13 people** (12 guests + you). Driver's food is covered by bata.

## Break-even (very important)
Fixed costs (TT, permit, tolls) don't reduce if fewer people come. Each missing guest saves only ~₹2,400 (his stay + food + ticket).

| Paying guests | Approx. cost | Revenue at ₹6,299 | Profit / Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 62,100 | 75,588 | **+13,500** |
| 11 | 59,700 | 69,289 | **+9,600** |
| 10 | 57,300 | 62,990 | **+5,700** |
| 9 | 54,900 | 56,691 | **+1,800** |
| 8 | 52,500 | 50,392 | **–2,100 (loss)** |

→ **Minimum 9–10 bookings** before you confirm the TT. If you get only 6–8 people, either increase price, take a smaller vehicle (Innova/Ertiga – different rate), or postpone.

## What's NOT included (tell guests clearly)
- Sunday dinner (or include it at +₹150)
- Personal shopping, extra snacks, horse riding, cycling, camera fees
- Anything due to weather, road blocks, landslides (extra km / stay)

## Payment & cancellation policy (suggested)
- **Booking advance: ₹2,000 per head (non-refundable)** – needed to pay TT & hotel advance.
- **Balance:** 5 days before the trip.
- Cancel 7+ days before: advance lost, balance refunded.
- Cancel within 7 days: no refund (can transfer seat to a friend).
- Trip cancelled by you (rain/red alert): full refund or shift to next date.

## Money tracking tips
- Keep a simple Google Sheet: name, phone, paid advance, paid balance, veg/non-veg, emergency contact.
- Take payments on UPI only (no cash confusion). Screenshot every payment.
- Keep **₹5,000 cash** with you on the trip for tolls/parking/emergencies (no network in some places).
- After the trip, write down real costs vs. planned – your next trip pricing will be accurate.
