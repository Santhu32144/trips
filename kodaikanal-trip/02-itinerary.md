# Itinerary – Friday Night to Sunday Night

## FRIDAY – Departure night

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 8:30 PM | Guide reaches pickup point. Check TT: seats clean, AC working, FASTag, permit, e-pass, first-aid kit, water cans loaded |
| 9:00 PM | Guest reporting time at **one** pickup point (e.g., Silk Board / Electronic City metro). Max 2 pickup points – more points = delay |
| 9:30 PM | **Depart** (sharp – tell guests 9:00 PM so you leave by 9:30) |
| 9:45 PM | Intro round in TT, trip rules, share emergency numbers, WhatsApp group check |
| 11:30 PM | Tea break – Krishnagiri highway dhaba |
| 11:45 PM – 5:00 AM | Night journey. Lights off, sleep. Karur break ~3:15 AM (restroom/tea) |
| ~4:30 AM | Near Dindigul: give motion-sickness tablets to those who need it |

## SATURDAY – Day 1 (Kodaikanal town + Forest circuit)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|

| 6:45 AM | **Silver Cascade Falls** (on the ghat, right by the road) – first photo stop, morning mist, tea |
| 7:30 AM | Reach stay. Freshen up (ask for **early check-in** or a common washroom room) |
| 8:30 AM | Breakfast |

| 10:30 AM | **Bryant Park** – flower garden |
| 11:15 AM | **Kodai Lake** – boating (pedal/row boat) or cycling round the lake (5 km) |
| 1:00 PM | Lunch near lake / Anna Salai |
| 2:00 PM | **Forest circuit** (all on one road, in this order): |
| | → **Pine Forest** (photo spot) |
| | → **Guna Caves (Devil's Kitchen)** – view from railing, entry inside is not allowed |
| | → **Pillar Rocks** – three giant rock pillars |
| | → **Moir Point** / Upper Lake View / **Green Valley View** |
| 5:30 PM | Back to town. Evening at **Kodai Lake market** – homemade chocolates, eucalyptus oil, tea, local cheese |
| 7:30 PM | Back to stay. **Bonfire** + games (antakshari, dumb charades) |
| 8:30 PM | Dinner |
| 10:30 PM | Lights out. Brief everyone: **5:45 AM wake-up** for Dolphin's Nose |

## SUNDAY – Day 2 (Vattakanal trek + checkout + return)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 5:45 AM | Wake up, tea |
| 6:15 AM | Drive to **Vattakanal** (~6 km). TT parks at Vattakanal; walk ~1–1.5 km downhill |

| 11:00 AM | **Chettiar Park** / quick shopping (last chocolates) |
| 12:00 PM | Lunch in Kodai town |
| **1:00 PM** | **DEPART Kodaikanal** (don't be late – this decides your Bangalore reach time) |
| 3:00 PM | Down the ghat, Batlagundu – tea |
| 6:30 PM | Karur / Namakkal – snack break |
| 8:30 PM | Dinner – Salem / Dharmapuri highway restaurant |
| 11:00–11:30 PM | **Reach Bangalore.** Drop at same pickup point |

## Optional / alternate spots (if time or interest)
- **Mannavanur Lake & sheep farm** (~35 km from town, grasslands, very scenic) – needs 4–5 hours. Swap with the Forest circuit only if group wants nature over "tourist points". Do it Saturday, not Sunday.
- **Poombarai village view** (terraced fields) – on the way to Mannavanur.
- **Bear Shola Falls** – good only in rainy season, short walk.
- **Berijam Lake** – needs forest dept permission, limited vehicles per day, closed some days. Ask locally.
- **Palani Murugan Temple** – if the group wants, descend via Palani ghat on Sunday. Adds ~1.5–2 hrs. Leave Kodai by 11:30 AM then.

## Rain plan (Oct–Dec is rainy)
- Coaker's Walk & Pillar Rocks may be fully covered in fog after noon – do views in the **morning**.
- If heavy rain: lake-side cafés, chocolate factory shops, Shenbaganur Museum, Kurinji temple, Pine Forest (still nice in mist).
- Never take the group near cliff edges in rain or heavy fog.
