# Trip Guide Checklist

Your job as guide = **plan, sell, coordinate, keep everyone safe, keep time, keep everyone happy, handle money.**

## 3–4 weeks before
- [ ] Fix the date. Check for long weekends (prices go up) and IMD weather / red alerts for Dindigul district.
- [ ] Get **2–3 TT quotes**. Ask every TT owner:
  - Seater: 12+1 or 13+1? Push-back seats? AC? Luggage carrier?
  - ₹18/km – is it AC? Minimum km per day (many have 250–300 km/day minimum)?
  - **Tolls, parking, TN permit – included or extra?**
  - Driver bata ₹500/day – how many days will he count? (If you reach Bangalore after midnight, some count a 4th day – agree in advance.)
  - Does TT have **AITP**, valid insurance, fitness certificate, FASTag?
  - Driver experienced in **ghat roads**? Has he done Kodaikanal before?
  - Get the final deal **in writing on WhatsApp**.
- [ ] Shortlist 2–3 stays. Confirm TT parking + bonfire + driver room + early check-in.
- [ ] Make poster + WhatsApp message (template below). Start taking bookings.

## 2 weeks before
- [ ] Reach **minimum 9–10 bookings** (break-even). If not, decide: postpone / smaller vehicle / increase price.
- [ ] Pay TT advance and hotel advance. Save receipts.
- [ ] Create WhatsApp group. Share itinerary + packing list + rules.
- [ ] Collect from each guest: full name, age, phone, **emergency contact**, ID proof copy, veg/non-veg, medical issues (asthma, BP, motion sickness).

## 3–5 days before
- [ ] Collect full balance.
- [ ] **Apply Kodaikanal e-pass** (epass.tnega.org). Share QR with driver.
- [ ] Confirm TN permit/border tax with TT owner.
- [ ] Re-confirm hotel (room count, check-in time, food menu).
- [ ] Check weather forecast. If red alert / landslide news on Kodai ghat – postpone, don't risk it.
- [ ] Get driver's name, phone, vehicle number → share in group.
- [ ] Buy first-aid kit, snacks, water cans.

## Trip day (Friday)
- [ ] Reach pickup point 30 min early. Inspect TT (tyres, AC, seat belts, cleanliness, spare tyre).
- [ ] Head count. Check every guest has ID.
- [ ] Brief rules (below). Share your number + driver number.
- [ ] Ensure the driver had sleep/rest during the day before the night drive.

## During the trip
- [ ] **Head count every time** the TT moves. Use a numbered list – each person shouts their number.
- [ ] Tell exact return time at every stop ("Back in TT at 11:20, not 11:30").
- [ ] Keep the 5 most important timings: depart Fri 9:30 PM, ghat 5:15 AM, forest circuit 2 PM, Dolphin's Nose 6:15 AM, **leave Kodai 1 PM Sunday**.
- [ ] Keep a "buddy system" – each person has a buddy, especially at Dolphin's Nose & Guna Caves.
- [ ] Make sure the **driver sleeps properly Saturday night**. No one disturbs him. Don't let him drink.
- [ ] Track expenses in your notebook immediately.
- [ ] Take group photos at every spot – these become your marketing for the next trip.

## Trip rules (read out in the TT)
1. Be on time. TT waits max 10 minutes.
2. No alcohol / smoking in the TT. No alcohol on treks or near cliffs.
3. Stay behind fences at Guna Caves, Dolphin's Nose, Coaker's Walk. No risky selfies.
4. No plastic in Kodaikanal. Don't litter.
5. Respect the driver – no "go faster" requests.
6. Inform the guide before going anywhere alone.
7. Respect each other; any harassment = removed from trip, no refund.

## After the trip
- [ ] Settle TT and driver bata (pay bata directly to the driver + small tip ₹200–500 if he did well).
- [ ] Post photos in group. Ask for **feedback & Google/Instagram reviews**.
- [ ] Write actual costs vs planned in [costing.csv](costing.csv).
- [ ] Note what went wrong → fix for next trip.

## Emergency numbers
- **All emergencies: 112**
- Ambulance: **108**
- Police: **100**
- Highway helpline (NHAI): **1033**
- Kodaikanal Government Hospital is in town – save its location on Google Maps.
- Save: TT owner number, driver number, hotel number, a backup TT/taxi contact in Kodaikanal.
