# Stay & Food

## Stay – 1 night (Saturday)

**What to look for**
- Group cottage / homestay / villa that can sleep **12 guests + guide (13 people)** (3–4 rooms, 3–4 sharing per room, extra mattresses ok).
- **Bonfire allowed** (many places in town don't allow it – confirm).
- **Parking for a TT** – very important; narrow roads in Kodai. Ask "Can a 12-seater Tempo Traveller reach the gate?"
- **Hot water (geyser)** – it's cold, guests will complain without it.
- **Driver room/dorm** – free or ₹300–500. Driver needs proper sleep before the return night drive.
- **Early check-in** (7:30 AM Saturday) – or at least one room/washroom to freshen up. Ask, and pay a little extra if needed.
- **Separate rooms for girls** if it's a mixed group.

**Good areas**
- Near Kodai Lake / Anna Salai – walkable to market, easy for evening.
- **Vattakanal** – backpacker vibe, close to Dolphin's Nose, cheaper, but narrow road (confirm TT access).
- Observatory Road / Lake Road – quieter cottages.

**How to book**
- Search on Google Maps / Booking.com / MakeMyTrip / Airbnb for "group cottage Kodaikanal", "homestay Kodaikanal 12 people".
- Call directly – you'll get better group rates than the website.
- Budget: **₹10,000–14,000 for the whole group per night** incl. bonfire (≈ ₹900–1,200 per head). Weekends and long weekends cost more.
- Pay 25–50% advance, get **written confirmation on WhatsApp** with: dates, room count, check-in time, inclusions (breakfast? bonfire? geyser? driver room?).

## Food plan (5 meals + night snacks)

| Meal | Where | Budget per head |
|---|---|---|
| Fri night snacks/tea | Krishnagiri dhaba (guests pay own, or you give a snack pack) | ₹50 |
| Sat breakfast | At stay or a town hotel (pongal, idli, dosa) | ₹120 |
| Sat lunch | Near lake / Anna Salai – meals or biryani | ₹250 |
| Sat dinner | At stay with bonfire (ask host to cook – veg & non-veg) | ₹300 |
| Sun breakfast | At stay | ₹120 |
| Sun lunch | Kodai town before leaving | ₹250 |
| Sun dinner | Highway restaurant near Salem/Dharmapuri | — (guests' own, or add ₹150) |

**Tips**
- Order food for a group in advance (call the restaurant 1 hour before) – saves 45 minutes.
- Ask guests at booking: **veg / non-veg / allergies**.
- Keep 1 water can (20 L) in the TT + guests carry their own bottles.
- Night snacks pack for TT (biscuits, chips, fruit) – ₹50 per head, guests love it.
