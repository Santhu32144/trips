# Places & Photo Spots

Entry fees are approximate – keep ₹300–350 per head budget for all tickets + boating.

## Main places (in the plan)

| Place | What it is | Time needed | Approx. fee | Best time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Silver Cascade Falls** | 180 ft waterfall right on the ghat road | 15–20 min | Free | Morning, monsoon |
| **Coaker's Walk** | 1 km paved cliff-side walkway, view of plains & valley | 45 min | ~₹20–30 | Before 11 AM (fog later) |
| **Bryant Park** | Botanical flower garden near lake | 30–45 min | ~₹30–50 | Morning |
| **Kodai Lake** | Star-shaped man-made lake, boating, cycling, horse ride | 1–1.5 hr | Boat ~₹200–500 per boat (30 min) | Late morning / evening |
| **Pine Forest** | Tall pine trees in rows – famous film spot | 30 min | Small fee | Afternoon light / mist |
| **Guna Caves (Devil's Kitchen)** | Deep caves seen in the movie *Guna* & *Manjummel Boys*. View from fenced area only | 30 min | ~₹20–30 | Afternoon |
| **Pillar Rocks** | Three 400 ft rock pillars | 20–30 min | ~₹10–20 | Clear-weather afternoon |
| **Moir Point / Green Valley View** | Valley viewpoints on the forest road | 20 min each | Small fee | Afternoon |
| **Dolphin's Nose (Vattakanal)** | Flat rock over a 6,600 ft deep valley – short trek | 2 hrs incl. walk | Free | **Sunrise / early morning** |
| **Echo Rock & Vattakanal Falls** | On the same Vattakanal trail | – | Free | Morning |
| **Kurinji Andavar Temple** | Murugan temple with Palani hills & Vaigai dam view | 30–40 min | Free | Morning |
| **Chettiar Park** | Small park, Kurinji flowers (bloom once in 12 yrs) | 20 min | Small fee | Anytime |

## Top photo spots (tell guests in advance so they dress nicely 😄)
1. **Pine Forest** – group photo between tall trees; mist makes it magical.
2. **Dolphin's Nose** – the edge rock shot (stay **2 steps back** from the edge – safety!).
3. **Coaker's Walk** – clouds below you on a clear morning.
4. **Kodai Lake** – evening boating & reflections.
5. **Silver Cascade** – full group photo with the falls.
6. **Pillar Rocks** – when clouds clear, the three pillars appear.
7. **Green Valley View** – wide valley panorama.

## Shopping
- **Homemade chocolates** – Kodai is famous for them (shops on Anna Salai & around lake). ₹100–150 per 100 g.
- **Eucalyptus oil, spices, tea**
- **Kodai cheese** (Kodai Cheese / local dairies)
- **Plums, pears, avocados** (season-wise) near the lake market

## Rules at Kodaikanal
- **Plastic ban** – no plastic water bottles (below 1 L) or carry bags. Carry steel/reusable bottles. Fines are charged at check posts and shops.
- Guna Caves – **do not cross the fence**. People have died there.
- Don't feed monkeys at Pillar Rocks / Guna Caves.
- Forest points close by ~5–5:30 PM.
